# Block 04: the voice lines

The bans in the s0mebody output style and `config/ai-slop-phrases.json` hold for every script, marketing included. On 8 Oct 2026 the bans were lifted for "marketing perspective" copy and Christian rejected all three results: "we still have to ban claude-speak."

## Each line

- Carries a person, a time, a number or a concrete object.
- Is something a person would say across a table to the viewer.
- Has one idea, and any second clause in it adds a fact.
- Puts the viewer in it with "your" where the claim is about him.
- Gives every number its unit and its owner, in the unit the viewer counts, which is often not the easiest one to show. The client: "contacts are ok but those are people, sellers care about how many conversations."
- Speaks every number that is on screen, or makes it explain itself. Blind readers asked what an unspoken "100 SELLERS · 50 BUYERS EACH" counted.
- Names a third person by the role word the viewer uses, "the seller", never he or she. A stress-test reader took "her" for the wrong person.
- Says plainly what the product does, with no coined compound of brand and noun. "Brand owner" got "what does brand owner mean?"
- Stays one TTS line when it is one spoken thought. Two sentences a person says in one breath, voiced as two clips, come back at two energies; Christian heard the jump between the first two lines of an intro (11 Oct). Merge them into one line, one take.

## Patterns that failed on 8 Oct, and what replaced them

| Failed line | Pattern | What worked |
|---|---|---|
| "already qualified, already matched, already written up" | three in a row | One clause per piece, on its own screen |
| "It doesn't have to be you." | a closing beat | "That leaves your team the owner meetings and the close." |
| "You open the file. You do not start it." | a mirrored pair | "Your first job is to check a file that is already built." |
| "Picture the next one arriving..." | a "Picture" opener | Open on a real scene happening now |
| "every sell-side firm does all four" | a claim with nothing behind it | "your firm does all four today" |
| "Then... Then... Then..." | three stages opened the same way | First, Next, Then, Last come |
| The same list said twice | a repeat | Point back to it: "asks each one those same questions" |
| "Most firms are not a fit, and you will know fast." | stiff when spoken | Describe the right buyer: "takes on firms that already close deals and want more of them" |

## Ratios

- Persuade copy wants about two "you" for every "we". Use the product's name in place of "we" to fix the ratio.
- Under 50 words per beat.
- About 150 spoken words per minute on paper, then measured on the real voice. On 8 Oct 2026 ElevenLabs Eric read the 331-word VSL at 204 words a minute, so the planned 2 minutes 10 seconds came out at 1 minute 40. Voice one line first, time it, and set the word budget from that.
- Write every pause into the script, in seconds: `[pause n]` before a line, `[n]` inside one, wherever the viewer should look at the picture. Voice at ElevenLabs `speed: 0.9` before the first render. On 13 Oct 2026 Christian heard Why Nations Fail Ep 1 as rushed: about 168 words a minute, 0.3 s gaps between lines, and no written pauses. Speed 0.9 plus written pauses fixed it without stretching the audio.

## Before handing a draft over

Run the linter until CLEAN. Read every line aloud once.

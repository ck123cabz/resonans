# Block 01: viewer, insight, idea

Source: Jason Murray's Big Idea Pyramid (`knowledge/corpus/jason-murray/analysis/core-knowledge.md`) and the jobs-to-be-done note (`04-resources/thinking-tools/jobs-to-be-done.md`).

## Fill these before anything else

| Slot | Rule | Client example (8 Oct 2026) |
|---|---|---|
| Viewer | One person in one moment, with a job title | A managing partner of a 12-person brokerage, on his phone between calls |
| Surface | Where it plays and where the viewer came from | YouTube, reached from a cold email link |
| Feeling | One feeling at the end, in the viewer's words | Relief: "they do the work I hate" |
| Insight | A human truth that explains a behaviour | A firm can only run as many deals as it has people for the prep |
| Idea | One sentence of what the whole video does with the insight | Show the four pieces of prep, then show them done by the system |

## Tests for the insight

- It explains why the viewer behaves a certain way. "Brokers need deals" is an observation; it fails.
- It holds a tension the viewer feels: a gap, a contradiction, a cost they carry.
- It is true of the viewer without the product in the room.
- It fits in one sentence a person could say to a colleague.

## When there is more than one viewer

Run the slots once per viewer. If one value carries all of them, keep one video and give each viewer one swap line near the end. For one client on 8 Oct the value was the infrastructure, so one video served the partner, the deal lead and the principal.

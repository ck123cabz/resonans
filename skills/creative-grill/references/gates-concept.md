# Gates 1 to 3: concept

All from Murray, core §2. Grill these before any craft question. Each gate lists the question,
the test, a weak answer, the drill for a weak answer, and the source.

## Gate 1: insight

- **Ask:** "People think X, but the reality is Y." Fill both halves. Write it plain: "we're not
  going to dress it up yet like a tagline or campaign. We just want the truth." ([D] S 2026-06-02)
- **Test:** it sounds "counterintuitive, uncomfortable, or like a contradiction at first", then
  the reader goes "ah ha". ([D:vis] AD26 frames)
- **Board traits**, four checks: rooted in human behaviour; not just a fact, it asks why;
  tension-driven, a gap or contradiction in people's lives; actionable, it opens an idea the
  brand or channel can own. ([D:vis] PIVOT frames)
- **Weak:** an observation. His examples: "the sky is blue", "people get hungry in the mornings
  so they need their breakfast". A research finding is also an observation until a motive shows.
- **Drill:** ask why, then ask why again. On the Coppertone line he says "if I wanted to make
  this even more intriguing I dig deeper". Observation and insight sit on one line; move right.
  ([D] LIVE, PIVOT)
- **Hold:** no insight, no further gates. Say so and stop the round.

## Gate 2: idea

- **Ask:** "So let's ___." The idea is "the verb what are we going to do with that insight":
  dramatize, contrast, flip. ([D] ADT, S 2026-06-16)
- **Then ask:** what is it called? A one-line name "show[s] that you can distill an idea down to
  a single line". ([D] PORT)
- **Test, legs:** name five executions under it. "if that big idea could ... have hundreds of
  mini ideas underneath it then it's a big idea". ([D] AD25) A name he can say in one line.
- **Board traits:** Simple & Clear; Emotional; Campaignable; Brand-Ownable. ([D:vis] AD26 frames)
- **Weak:** one execution posing as an idea. "what if we just put a sneaker in Time Square well
  that's not really a big idea that's just coming up with an execution". ([D] AD25)
  A format ("a reel with text on screen") is also an execution.
- **Drill:** ask what the execution does to the insight, as a verb. If no verb comes, go back
  to gate 1; the insight is usually still an observation.
- **Worked example to show him** when he is stuck, Murray's Nike "Rip the Script" reading:
  insight, football feels staged but the best moments are instinct; idea, "rip up every playbook
  football is being told to follow". ([D] S 2026-06-16) It is his reverse-engineering of Nike.

## Gate 3: size

- **Ask:** where does this sit on the Idea Sizing Chart? ([D:vis] AD26 frames)

| Row | Group |
|---|---|
| Business Strategy & Positioning | |
| Branding | |
| Platform | Big Ideas |
| Campaign | |
| Execution | |
| One-off | Small Ideas |
| Tactics | |
| Crap | No ideas |

- **Why it matters for the rest:** a platform is "a single uh message a single line" that many
  campaigns run under. A series of videos is a campaign or a platform; one reel is an execution
  under it. The bigger the row, the more gate 5's threading rule matters. ([D] AD25)
- **Test:** big is scale, not quality. "Not every big idea is a great idea." Grade quality at
  gates 1 and 2, size here. ([D] S 2026-06-16)
- **Weak:** a one-off called a campaign because it has several posts.
- **Drill:** ask what the second and third pieces would be if this one did well. If they are the
  same piece again with new words, it is a one-off or a tactic.

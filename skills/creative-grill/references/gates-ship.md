# Gate 7 and the borrowed gates

Gate 7 is Murray, core §8. Gates W and E are not from him and say whose they are.

## Gate 7: ship

- **Ask:** what is the hook? On a reel it has three parts: a title that triggers emotion or
  curiosity, a visual that signals who it is for, a first spoken line. ([D] HOOK)
- **Test:** "your hook should be clear not clever". It is "aligned with the story's idea
  otherwise you're going to grab someone's attention but there's nothing to hold on to".
  The hook comes from the idea, which comes from the insight. ([D] BLUEPRINT, HOOK)
- **His five attributes:** sparks curiosity; reframes something familiar; instantly
  understandable; hits a nerve; aligned with the idea. ([D] HOOK)
- **His formulas** to offer as options: a surprising question; a counterintuitive truth; a common
  mistake exposed; a bold claim. ([D] BLUEPRINT)
- **Weak:** a gimmick that startles and does not hold. A hook he could put on any video.
- **Drill:** read the hook, then the gate 2 idea. If the hook could open a different video, it
  is not aligned. Rewrite it from the insight.
- **Ask:** where is the finish line? "make the end of your guaranteed available time the finish
  line". If the time is not guaranteed, shrink the piece: "a doodle not a painting a comic strip
  not a graphic novel". ([D] FINISH)
- **Weak finish lines,** his three: when it is perfect, when it meets my standards, when it is
  500 pages long.
- **For a series:** "I think about my series and pillars like ad campaigns". The series name is
  the gate 3 platform line; each episode is a campaign or an execution under it. ([D] ADT)

## Gate W: words

Runs when the piece has a voiceover, a script or a caption.

- **Source 1, Dan Koe** (`/lens dan-koe ask`, `retrieval-index/writing-frameworks.md`, "Hook
  construction"). A hook balances relevance, awareness and effort. Its six building blocks: Big
  Problem, Big Benefit, Big Idea, Transformation Process, Time Frame, Negative Personal
  Experience. Pick the strongest combination. The corpus flags the triad as close to Hormozi's
  value equation; say so when you cite it.
- **Ask:** which two of the six blocks does the opening line carry?
- **Test:** the gate 7 hook still passes Murray's "clear not clever" after the blocks are added.
  Where Koe and Murray pull apart, Murray's test wins inside this skill, because the idea is
  graded by his tree.
- **Source 2, `writing-video-scripts`.** For a script, hand over here. Gates 1 and 2 fill its
  block 01 table (viewer, surface, feeling, insight, idea); it runs from step 3. Its own gates
  hold: segment table and first line shown before the rest.
- **Repo rule:** every voice line passes `scripts/ai_slop_lint.py`. Koe's "not X, Y" exclusion
  hook is banned by that filter; quote it, do not write it.

## Gate E: edit

Runs when there is footage. Source: the `short-form-edit` and `video-storytelling` skills in
`content-studio/`, built from Nate Herk's HyperFrames kit and reviews of our own builds.

- **Ask, the first gate of short-form-edit:** "In the first 3 seconds is someone convinced they
  need to watch until the end?" A specific reason to stay, paid off inside the video. Effects and
  polish come after it.
- **Ask:** which gate 5 ingredient shows in the edit? Pace, hold against movement, hard cuts
  against transitions, loops are Murray's Motion & Editing line; pick one and give its reason.
- **Test:** mute the reel. If the gate 4 answer was "the picture carries it", the message
  survives muted. If it was "the words carry it", captions carry it muted.
- **Weak:** effects added for retention with no line back to the idea. video-storytelling's
  mute-test review scored a first build 7 out of 10 for exactly this: viewers spent attention
  decoding graphics that carried nothing.
- **Hand off** to `short-form-edit` or `edit-video` with the brief as input.

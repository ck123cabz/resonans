# Gates 4 to 6: craft

All from Murray, core §3 and §5. Run only after gates 1 and 2 are passed. The full menu of 61
ingredients is `knowledge/corpus/jason-murray/analysis/retrieval-index/ingredient-menu.md`.

## Gate 4: who carries the message

- **Ask:** does the copy carry the message, or does the picture? Murray draws one line: visual
  feeling on the left, visual solution on the right. "There is no right or wrong." ([D] ADT)
- **Left, visual feeling:** words, voice or dialogue tell the message; the visuals make you feel
  it. Test: reset the type in Helvetica. "yes we would get the same message but we wouldn't feel
  it in the same way." What is lost is the ingredient doing the work. ([D] ADT)
- **Right, visual solution:** the pairing of images is the message and copy shrinks to "a
  tagline or a sort of button". Tests: "endless subjects you could slot in"; and "If a viewer has
  to pause to decode the metaphor, the device has failed." ([D] ADT, MAD 2026-04-08)
- **Weak:** "both". Every piece sits somewhere on the line; he places each ad by speech.
- **Weak, right side:** copy that says what the picture shows. His word is "see-say".
- **Drill:** mute it, or cover the words. If the message survives, it sits right. If it dies,
  it sits left, and gate 5 grades ingredients by whether you feel the words.

## Gate 5: ingredients

- **Ask:** which two or three ingredients stand out? "there might be a lot of ingredients to make
  a dish but there's usually like two to three standout flavors". ([D] ADT)
- **Ask, for each:** what is the reason? "a good art director always has a reason. Even when the
  honest reason is 'it just looks right,' you still gotta walk stakeholders through why."
  ([D] MAD 2026-07-06)
- **Ask, for a campaign or platform row:** which one ingredient threads every touchpoint, "an
  ingredient that can live in an out of home ad and within an actual in-person IRL popup".
  For a video series: what repeats in every episode and every reel. ([D] ADT)
- **Ask:** what is the key visual, the one image that tells the idea? It carries the idea's name,
  never only the logo. ([D] PORT, LIVE)
- **Test:** each ingredient "serve[s] the idea" and helps you "feel and experience the insight
  for yourself". ([D] ADT) Three at most.
- **Weak:** a list of five or more, or an ingredient chosen for its look with no line back to
  the insight.
- **Drill:** for each ingredient, finish the sentence "this makes you feel <the insight> because
  ___". Cut the ones that cannot finish it.
- **Menu headings to offer as options** (core §3): Color & Light, Composition & Framing, Imagery,
  Casting & Talent, Styling & Set, Type & Graphics, Visual References, Motion & Editing, Mood,
  Constraints, Perspective & Intent.
- **Music** is an ingredient off the menu: "one of the most important creative ingredients And
  it's not even visual". His three moves: the Mismatch (calm footage, aggressive track), the
  Amplifier (match the emotion and turn it up), the Sonic Signature (repeat a motif across
  videos). Pick from one artist or one world; avoid "anything that feels generic or
  interchangeable". He said this inside a Musicbed sponsor read. ([D] ADT, HOOK)
- **Photo direction** detail (flash, lenses, shot lists) is his guest Garrett King's. Label it
  when used.
- **Handoff:** a named technique goes to `visual-techniques brief` for the shortlist. Rule 25:
  too obvious, try figure-ground reversal; means nothing, genre quotation; too literal, form
  substitution. ([D] S 2026-09-30)

## Gate 6: references

- **Work already built** (a render, an earlier episode): read its frames as the references.
  The gate's job then is naming what the next piece keeps, at level 3, by menu line.
- **Ask for his saves first** (moodboard, screenshots, earlier drafts) and read each at Murray's
  three levels: like or dislike; how it feels, as a simile ("this maybe feels like a Michel
  Gondry film"); the ingredient that made it feel that way. ([D] TOT)
- **Ask:** whose taste is this for? Mainstream, cultural, counter, elite, commercial. "your
  taste doesn't matter if you can't make it palatable to others ... Whose taste?"
  ([D] S 2026-05-01)
- **Test, per image:** "if I can't answer why an image is there it doesn't make the cut". The
  set looks "made by one person with one intention". ([D] MOOD)
- **Test, per image:** who can make this? Trace it past the post to the photographer or director.
  ([D] TOT)
- **Test, the set:** "put your client hat on and think is anything going to be misinterpreted".
  For his own content, the client is the viewer. ([D] MOOD)
- **Weak:** "I like the vibe". That is level 1. Push to level 3: name the ingredient.
- **Drill:** for each save ask why he saved it, whether he actually likes it, and what about it.
- **Silent spots:** Murray gives no image count for a board and no rule for photography against
  illustration or CG. Say so when asked.

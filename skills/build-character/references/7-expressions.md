# Step 7: Expressions

## Grill

- Which features can act? If the identity hides the eyes (Leon's visor), the character's own anatomy carries the
  face: for a penguin the beak is the mouth (Christian, 25 Oct), never a mouth drawn under it.
- Which emotions do the reels need?

## Build

- `rig/expr.js`: each expression sets the acting parts (Leon: beak state, blush, head tilt, face sink or lift,
  crown droop or ride, sweat, tear, a breath puff).
- `sheets/exprsheet.js`: every expression full size, then at phone size (head about 120 px), then a few in side view.

## Sheet

The expression sheet. Then the fresh reader, judging at phone size.

## Checks

- Every expression needs its own mouth shape, big enough to read at phone size; blush alone did not carry Leon's smile.
- Anything that reads as a second mouth goes (Leon's under-face shade curve).
- List the look-alikes and fix each pair until it parts (sunk and tired, sigh and talk at the first pass).
- Drops sit on plain colour with a dark outline, off any busy area: Leon's sweat vanished into the visor's blue band.
- Talking and other motion-only faces are judged in motion, not on the sheet.

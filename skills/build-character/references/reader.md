# The fresh reader

One reader per sheet, never told the agent's reasoning or fixes. Use Opus: the routing rubric has no row for this
job, and on Leon it caught faults the builder missed at every sheet (scores and claims are in the character's brief).

Brief, filled in per sheet:

> You are a cartoon art director. Open the sheet `<path>` and the style target frames `<paths>`. The character is
> meant to pass as a character of `<style target>` wearing fixed traits: `<traits>`. Do not critique the traits.
> Judge only whether it is drawn the way the target draws its characters: `<what this step checks>`. Report a score
> out of 10, every mismatch ranked most visible first with where it is and what the target does instead, and what
> already matches. Under 400 words. Do not edit files.

Measure every claim on the sheet before acting on it. Report wrong claims to the user with the numbers.

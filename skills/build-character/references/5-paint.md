# Step 5: Paint

## Grill

- Which traits from the identity source carry over, and in what colours?
- Where the identity's colour is louder than the show's palette, which wins? Leon: the show's softer green (#5ec46e).

## Build

- `rig/parts.js` draws the accessories; `rig/front.js` and `rig/side.js` assemble the views. Flat fills, one hard
  shadow tone per colour, the outline in the target's line colour and weight.
- `sheets/paintsheet.js`: target frame, front, side, front over the frame at 55%, and front at 360 px wide.

## Sheet

The paint sheet. Then the fresh reader (`reader.md`).

## Checks

- Measure the line weight on the target frame. Leon went 3.5% of W, then 2.2%, and landed at 1.6% in dark navy.
- Body colour wraps round the face as a mask, the way the show does it. A white face with thin green slivers read
  off-model.
- Accessories stay few and big enough to read at 360 px wide: Leon's crown went from 10 small flowers to 7 big ones.
- Paint shades and patterns in flat bands. Gradient rings read as pasted-on.
- Measure every reader claim before acting on it. The reader said Leon was two-thirds her width; the sheet measured
  his belly at 309 px and hers at 304.

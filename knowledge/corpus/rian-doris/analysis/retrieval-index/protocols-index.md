# Rian Doris Retrieval Index — Master Protocols Index

Every named protocol, framework, rule, law and system in the corpus, one line each, alphabetical.
Grep the name here, then open the home file for the full treatment. Compiled from the lane blocks
of `pass-1-extraction.md` (1,204 signals across 60 files) and q60's own framework sweep, with the
merge map and the four functional families from `pass-2-reconciliation.md`.

**Home files:** `goals-and-time.md` · `attention-and-load.md` · `motivation-and-dopamine.md` ·
`discipline-and-persistence.md` · `flow-mechanics.md` · `physiology-and-recovery.md` ·
`leverage-and-people.md` · `biography-and-business.md`. Every figure in the corpus is also indexed
by value in `numbers-index.md`.

**Family column.** Pass 2 found one argument under all forty-odd protocols: attention is the scarce
resource, drained by accumulated load and fragmented by interruption, and every protocol either
removes load, protects a block, matches work to biological capacity, or restores capacity after it
is spent. Entries that are definitions, laws or diagnoses rather than executable moves carry `—`.

**Provenance.** `[D]` unless marked. `[M:name]` inside a definition means the construct belongs to
an outside authority per q59's citation map, even where the file it appears in states it flat.
`[G]` marks his own outcome standing behind it.

## Notes on the map

**Names that collide and must never be merged.** Three unrelated constructs share the term *flow
channel* (challenge vs skill, blood sugar, arousal). Three unrelated instruments share audit-shaped
names (the 30-year Temporal Audit, the 15-minute-granularity time log, the calendar audit for real
work hours). *Certainty Windows* carries two incompatible scales, months and minutes, under one
name. *Preoccupation* (q23) and *rumination* (q27) share a substrate and take opposite
prescriptions.

**Protocols that compete for the same block with no integration rule.** Boring Breaks wants 45 on
and 5 off, a certainty window wants an unbroken 10 to 30 minutes, and the 3-Hour Rule wants the
first 3 hours before anything else. The corpus never says how they nest.

**Two protocols answering the same question by different routes.** Boring Breaks (q31) and the five
intrinsic motivators (q32) both answer "how do I make hard work feel like TikTok," and neither
mentions the other.

**Where a figure is disputed**, the table carries both readings in bold rather than picking one.
With no upload dates in the corpus, none of these can be read as revision rather than
inconsistency.

## Early Stage: Google Ads (Paid Search)

**Source:** Signal 12

### When to Use
Early-stage B2B. Need cash flow before building content flywheel.

### Why Google Ads First
- Captures existing demand (people already searching for your category)
- Less creative work than paid social (no display ads to design)
- Basic copywriting is enough
- Fast feedback on whether demand exists

### Budget Formula
1. What's one customer worth? (e.g., $200/month × 10 months avg = $2,000 LTV)
2. How much will I pay per customer? (e.g., $600 acceptable CAC)
3. How many customers per month? (e.g., 10)
4. Monthly budget = $600 × 10 = $6,000

### Caveat for an outbound agency
This works if M&A buyers are actively searching for lead gen services. If they're not (likely — the category may not have search volume), Google Ads doesn't apply and LinkedIn/content is the play.

## Growth Stage: LinkedIn Thought Leadership Ads

**Source:** Signals 13, 14

### Mechanics
1. Get employees posting on LinkedIn (builds organic trust)
2. Boost top-performing employee posts as thought leadership ads
3. Run with impression goal (not conversion) — cheapest LinkedIn ad format
4. Spread messaging across new markets and geographies
5. Scrape engagers from boosted posts
6. Run outbound from sales team on those engagers (warm outbound)
7. Retarget with product-based bottom-of-funnel ads to convert

### Campaign Structure
| Layer | Audience | Goal | CTA |
|-------|----------|------|-----|
| Top of funnel | Large, ICP-adjacent | Awareness, top of mind | None — just impressions |
| Bottom of funnel | Retargeting + industry/title splits | Demos, meetings | Direct booking CTA |

No middle funnel on LinkedIn. Gromer says the distinction between top and middle is too blurry on that platform.

### Messaging Rule
Bottom-of-funnel ads must be specific to job title and industry. "Find new CFO leads" outperforms "find new C-level leads." The specificity signals relevance.

### ColdIQ Benchmark
Michel Lieben: $5,100 spent in July 2025 on thought leadership ads → 155,000 impressions at $33 CPM → "massive impact" in meetings booked within three weeks. $472K at 3x ROI for clients across six months using LinkedIn Ads API automation.

## Integration: Ads + Outbound (Services Company Play)

**Source:** Signal 8

The two ad strategies feed the services company playbook:

1. Build a 10K lead list
2. Run impression-based thought leadership ads on that list for 1-2 weeks
3. Clay pulls the top 200 by intent/ICP
4. Warm outbound on the 200

Budget can be minimal (impression-based thought leadership ads are cheap). The ROI is in the conversion rate improvement on the outbound, not in the ads themselves.

## 2. Provenance gaps

**The corpus audits itself, and both audits are incomplete.** q67 is a citation map naming 39
thinkers; q68 is a coinage audit covering 15 of his terms. Pass 1 found:

- **q67 undercounts sources by roughly a quarter.** Absent from it but attributed elsewhere in the
  corpus: Tim Ferriss, Austin Kleon, Wilson Mizner, Parkinson, the Zettelkasten and Smart Notes
  tradition, Billy Wilson, Matt Ogus, the Prussian education model, and the biohacker "Grimhood".
- **q68 misses the largest cases**: Human 3.0, the NPC-to-Synthesizer progression,
  enjoyment-versus-pleasure, digital leverage, and the James Clear borrowing behind the
  identity-evidence list.
- **Neither covers** just-in-time versus just-in-case learning (PKM tradition), the premortem
  (Gary Klein), "Eternal Markets" (standard direct-response taxonomy), memes as replicating units
  (Dawkins, behind Conceptual Survival), or 1,000 True Fans (Kevin Kelly's, credited in the corpus
  to Ryan Deiss as the transmission vector).

**Working rule:** q68 governs the 15 terms it names, q67 governs everything else, and silence in
either is not evidence that no source exists.

**The credit tally is better than the framing suggests.** q68's own numbers: 9 explicitly credited,
1 explicitly disclaimed, 3 partially credited, 2 fully uncredited. He credits far more than he
hides, and one case is unusually candid: he records that the 4-Hour Workday came from misreading
*The 4-Hour Workweek* in college and keeping the misreading.

**The qualification is a file-class pattern.** Files about a thinker's model credit generously and
by full name. Files about a tactic strip the same material of attribution. Csikszentmihalyi is
never named across twelve philosophy files despite carrying three of the frameworks in them; five
of those twelve name nobody at all. A reader meeting the material through one tactical video
receives Wilber's, Cook-Greuter's and Csikszentmihalyi's work with no attribution.

**One factual error, carried as an error.** He credits Beck and Cowan for a colour list that is not
theirs: red, amber, orange, green, teal. Spiral Dynamics runs beige, purple, red, blue, orange,
green, yellow, turquoise, and **amber and teal are Wilber's altitude colours**. He has blended two
developmental systems and attributed the result to one. Whether he renders Wilber and Cook-Greuter
faithfully elsewhere is untested, and this error is reason to check.

## 3. Evidence gaps

**Grounding is his strength.** 182 `[G]` signals, the largest layer of the three corpora in this
family and four times `rian-doris`. The costliest is self-reported: **$3,000 borrowed from his
father and lost entirely** on blue-light glasses advertised to an audience he did not have. He
names that loss as the reason he teaches audience before product, and that rule organises the
whole business model.

**The direction is the problem.** Doris grounds costs and asserts benefits. Koe grounds both, in
detail, and every benefit number is his own, in a business whose product is teaching the method
that produced them. Stated plainly rather than treated as disqualifying.

**Two artifacts to carry:**
- His rule is $0.50 to $1.00 per follower per month. Four worked examples sit at exactly $1.00,
  the ceiling of his own range. The 500-follower example works out at $6.00, six times that
  ceiling. No example anywhere sits at $0.50.
- Three population claims arrive with no method and do not agree with each other: 80-90% of the
  population at Human 1.0/2.0, 80-95% of social media users at conventional stages, 95% of mental
  energy gone in 2-4 hours.

**Unsupported strong claims**, enumerated by the corpus's own sweep: an AI timeline sourced to
tweets from tech CEOs and treated as near-certain; the claim that public schools were explicitly
created to reinforce obedience and are corporate-funded to keep citizens sick, gestured at the
Prussian model with no further evidence; blue light "definitely" contributing to chronic disease
and a "solar callus" preventing sunburn, both offered while he says he does not know the science
and refers viewers to a Twitter biohacker; and a guaranteed 95%+ profit margin against his own
reported 90% take-home.

**No failed adopter appears anywhere.** Six documented failures, all his own and all pre-success.

## 4. Population validity

The advice assumes conditions the corpus never names. Concentrated in the sweep and confirmed
across lanes:

- Signing a lease at **twice what you can afford**, or spending savings on coaching, to trigger
  fight-or-flight
- Disappearing for six months; working 12 to 16 hours and forgetting to eat; daily 90-minute walks
- Mastering coding, copywriting, psychology and philosophy in 6 to 12 months on YouTube
- Treating anxiety and depression as symptoms of "pursuing society's goals"

**All four describe his own conditions at 22**: childless, sharing rent at $250 to $400 with seven
housemates, no dependents, parental credit available, and able to absorb a college coding
curriculum in three weeks. The advice is his circumstances generalised, and the lens must gate the
financial-risk material rather than repeat it.

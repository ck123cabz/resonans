# Retrieval Index — Frameworks

Every named framework, model, law, matrix, ladder and coined term in the corpus, one row each,
alphabetical. Grep this file first to find out what exists, then load the home file for the full
treatment. 186 rows.

**Credit tally from his own coinage audit (q68): 9 explicitly credited · 1 explicitly disclaimed ·
3 partially credited · 2 fully uncredited.** He credits far more than he hides. The accurate frame
is renaming with attribution.

## How to read the provenance column

| Marker | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `[D]` | his own, no outside model found underneath |
| `[D+M:name]` | his name over someone else's architecture. The name is his, the model is not. Never collapse to either half |
| `[M:name]` | a straight relay under the source's own name, nothing renamed |
| `[I]` | inferred by the ingestion, not stated in the corpus |
| `⚠` | a defect, conflict or gate that travels with the entry |

**Two senses of `[D]`, kept apart.** q68 audits 15 terms he presents as coinages, and Pass 2
corrects the profile against it: of the five items the profile called pure `[D]`, only the
**2-Hour Content Ecosystem** and **PPP** survive. That verdict covers the audited coinages, not the
whole index. Most `[D]` rows below carry the marker because no outside model was found underneath,
and the working rule forbids reading that silence as proof none exists: **q68 governs the 15 terms
it names, q67 governs everything else.** A third term, the Trust Matrix, is uncredited in q68 but
was never on the profile's list, so it sits outside the surviving pair.

**The credit column answers a different question from the provenance column.** Provenance records
what sits underneath. Credit records whether he says so, and the two diverge by file class: files
about a thinker's model credit generously and by full name, files about a tactic strip the same
material of attribution. Csikszentmihalyi carries the Focus Matrix, the Intensity phase and Gamify
Your Life, and is named in none of the three files that teach them.

**q67 undercounts sources by roughly a quarter.** Nine attributed names appear elsewhere in the
corpus and are absent from the citation map: Ferriss, Kleon, Mizner, Parkinson, the
Zettelkasten and Smart Notes tradition, Billy Wilson, Matt Ogus, the Prussian education model, and
the biohacker "Grimhood". The rows below merge them in.

**Excluded on purpose.** Software features that carry no transferable method, and the ~45 named
apps and services, live in `tools-named.md`. Every figure lives in `numbers-index.md`. Every
verbatim hook, prompt and example line lives in `templates-index.md`.

## Rules this file obeys

- **Nothing is rounded. Ranges stay ranges.** A figure given two ways keeps both renderings.
- **Twelve quantities conflict** (Pass 2 §3.4). Every competing value gets its own row and its own
  source, marked `⚠ conflicts`. No winner is picked anywhere in this file.
- **The revenue total is never computed.** The five years sum to roughly $6.9M against his stated
  "a little over $5 million". Both figures are recorded as stated and the arithmetic stops there.
- `⚠ variant` marks a smaller divergence outside Pass 2's twelve: a second rendering, a rule stated
  at two resolutions, a figure rounded differently in a second file. Nine of these exist, across
  fourteen rows, and they are listed at the foot of the file.
- `⚠ no method` marks a claim with no study, researcher or method behind it. Four figures carry it,
  across five rows, since the 95% depletion claim is indexed in two groups. The three population
  claims among them do not agree with each other.
- **Two product names are not measurements.** They sit in their own section at the bottom and
  appear in no table above it.

Provenance: `[D]` his own prescription · `[G]` backed by his own reported outcome ·
`[M:name]` relayed from a named source · `[I]` inferred by the ingestion · `[A]` a stated absence.

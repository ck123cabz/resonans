# Concept and spec process

How Jason Murray takes a spec campaign from a blank page to a portfolio page, then every worked example he gives as insight, idea and execution, then his examples for each row of the Idea Sizing Chart.

File paths are relative to `knowledge/corpus/jason-murray/.raw/`. Quotes from captions keep the caption's spelling and missing punctuation.

## The spec campaign process, step by step

He never lists these as numbered steps. Each step below is something he says or shows. The order follows his Project Checklist and the way his course runs: "the most important part for him was the first two weeks of just really you know internalizing what is the big idea before executing". [D] `video/art-direction-techniques-you-can-use.md`. The numbering is the corpus builder's, [I].

### Step 1. Know why you are making fake work

- "You can't get hired without the work, and you can't get the work without getting hired." [D] `newsletter/2026-08-14-how-to-prove-you-re-an-art-director-mad-010.md`
- "You make spec work (fake work). It's the number one way creatives get into this industry." [D] same file.
- Three things to prove, in his order: think conceptually, think at scale, translate the idea into visual solutions. [D] same file.
- His proof that it works is Ian Hagen's Dollar Shave Club campaign: "None of that is real He didn't actually work for Dollar Shave Club He just proved he could." [D] `video/how-to-craft-an-art-director-portfolio.md`

### Step 2. Pick the brand

- Pick "a globally recognized brand ideally so that you don't have to do a lot of the education". [D] `video/how-to-craft-an-art-director-portfolio.md`
- In August 2026 he wrote: "you make fake campaigns for the biggest brands in the world. You show them what you can do EXACTLY how you would do it for them." [D] `newsletter/2026-08-14-how-to-prove-you-re-an-art-director-mad-010.md`
- In 2025 he passed on the opposite advice: "don't make spec ads for Apple because you're never going to make fake ads that are better than the real ads". [D] `video/how-to-become-an-art-director-in-2025.md`
- He does not settle the two. They fit as: a known brand, and one whose real advertising you can beat. [I]
- The bar in all three sources: "making it look like it could be a real campaign and maybe even cooler and better than what's actually being made". [D] `video/how-to-become-an-art-director-in-2025.md`
- Briefs can come from student award archives. The One Club Young Ones grid is on his board. [D:vis] `frames/how-to-pivot-to-art-direction-with-no-experience.md`

### Step 3. Find the insight

- Definition: "an insight is a revelation or it's an unspoken human truth it's one of those things that when you say it it changes the way you see the world or it changes the way you see yourself". [D] `video/how-to-become-an-art-director-in-2026.md`
- The form to write it in: "People think X, but the reality is Y." [D] `shorts/2026-06-16-how-creative-directors-create-big-ideas-MhMhug5xo9E.md`
- Write it plain first: "we're not going to dress it up yet like a tagline or campaign. We just want the truth." [D] `shorts/2026-06-02-the-big-idea-blueprint-for-creatives-T2CyDwJCaGg.md`
- Four traits on his board: Rooted in Human Behavior, Not Just a Fact, Tension-Driven, Actionable. [D:vis] `frames/how-to-pivot-to-art-direction-with-no-experience.md`
- When a line is still an observation, ask why. On Rachel Holmes's Coppertone line he says: "that's a pretty great observation and that's what the research shows but if I wanted to make this even more intriguing I dig deeper". [D] `video/art-director-reacts-to-portfolios-live.md`
- Mark Pollard's rewrite, which Jason credits to him: "one of his techniques of making observations feel more like an insight is just write it in a more savage way". [D] `video/how-to-pivot-to-art-direction-with-no-experience.md`. The technique is [X:Mark Pollard].
- His test for the result: "is that a truth that sounds like a contradiction". [D] same file.
- NotebookLM's "five-step insight process" is Gemini's assembly. He never lists five steps. [M]

### Step 4. Write the idea as a verb

- "Then the idea is what we do with that truth What is our creative formula that will help our audience experience that insight for themselves". [D] `video/art-direction-techniques-you-can-use.md`
- "Big ideas need verbs. We could dramatize something. We could contrast something. We could flip something." [D] `shorts/2026-06-16-how-creative-directors-create-big-ideas-MhMhug5xo9E.md`
- His students write it as "So let's…". Connor McLoughlin's slide: "So let's reframe the elements as a playdate with the outside world." [D:vis] `frames/art-direction-techniques-you-can-use.md`
- The formula "If [Insight], then we will [Idea]" is NotebookLM's wording. It is not in the transcripts. [M]

### Step 5. Give the idea a name

- "Give your project a title as in the idea has a name the campaign concept has a name That's going to show that you can distill an idea down to a single line". [D] `video/how-to-craft-an-art-director-portfolio.md`
- Titles on screen: "Playdate", "Later Is Now", "Embrace Your Bottle Scars", "The Portion Pledge", "Decrypting Realtor Code". [D:vis] frames for the three videos.

### Step 6. Check the size of the idea

- "Not every big idea is a great idea. We're talking scale." [D] `shorts/2026-06-16-how-creative-directors-create-big-ideas-MhMhug5xo9E.md`
- The legs test: "if that big idea could could have you know legs like a centipede have hundreds of mini ideas underneath it then it's a big idea". [D] `video/how-to-become-an-art-director-in-2025.md`
- The counter-example: "what if we just put a sneaker in Time Square well that's not really a big idea that's just coming up with an execution". [D] same file.
- Luke Sullivan's two platform tests, which Jason reads off his board as Sullivan's: "It only qualifies as a platform if it fits on a Post-It note." and "It starts talking and won't shut up." [D:vis] `frames/how-to-become-an-art-director-in-2026.md`. Content is [X:Luke Sullivan].
- Four traits of a Big Idea on his board: Simple & Clear, Emotional, Campaignable, Brand-Ownable. [D:vis] same file.

### Step 7. Come up with executions in volume

- "just try not to come up with great ideas pour your subconscious out just come up with lots of ideas and a quantity of ideas". [D] `video/answering-every-aspiring-art-director-question-ye-concert-reaction.md`
- He works in sprints: "set a timer for 15 minutes and I force myself to just pour out every subconscious thought". [D] same file. NotebookLM's "30-to-60-minute timers" comes from a different answer.
- He sorts in a second sitting: "pour out all the ingredients that you can think of. And then in another session you can start to piece it together". [D] `video/the-art-direction-of-zendaya-x-spike-jonze.md`
- Push the strongest one further. Of Snickers he says "they pushed it to the edge of Madness", a phrase he credits to Jason Bagley. [D] `video/how-to-become-an-art-director-in-2025.md`. Phrase is [X:Jason Bagley].

### Step 8. Choose the look and feel

- "Once we have our concept the art director's job is to bring it to life". Ingredients come in here. [D] `video/art-direction-techniques-you-can-use.md`
- Keep to a few: "there's usually like two to three standout flavors standout ingredients" and "You need to have some restraint". [D] same file.
- Decide where the work sits on his line. Copy tells the message and the visuals support it (Visual Feeling), or the pairing of images is the message (Visual Solution). "There is no right or wrong". [D] same file.
- A good idea line already hints at the look. For Nike he reads "script-inspired visual ingredients, like movie sets, Hollywood actors, reality TV" out of "Rip the Script". [D] `shorts/2026-06-16-how-creative-directors-create-big-ideas-MhMhug5xo9E.md`
- Write the look down in words. "Without a clearly articulated vision, you get mismatch luggage." [D] `shorts/2025-03-17-the-language-of-art-direction-5-tGRNcDsIk.md`

### Step 9. Thread one ingredient across the touchpoints

- "You'll try to find an ingredient that can be threaded across these channels Ideally an ingredient that can live in an out of home ad and within an actual in-person IRL popup". [D] `video/art-direction-techniques-you-can-use.md`
- How many: his project table says "Try for 4-10 different touch-points" and his checklist says "4-6+ touchpoints/executions". [D:vis] `frames/how-to-craft-an-art-director-portfolio.md`. Both agree on a floor of four.
- Channels he lists: social, out of home, TV, an in-real-life piece, a stunt, an activation, print, an app. [D:vis] same file.
- Some pieces carry only the look: "Some touch points or content might just borrow look and feel, but not spend the time hammering the message." [D] `shorts/2026-06-16-how-creative-directors-create-big-ideas-MhMhug5xo9E.md`
- Jaya Schreihart's boarding pass runs through subway posters, a billboard, Instagram stories and a ticket-booth pop-up. Connor McLoughlin's cut-out letters run through print, out of home and a rain-room activation. [D:vis] `frames/art-direction-techniques-you-can-use.md`

### Step 10. Make a key visual or lockup

- "An optional thing but I highly recommend always having a key visual or a lockup That's some sort of a key visualizing a single image that represents the entire campaign". [D] `video/how-to-craft-an-art-director-portfolio.md`
- His model is Nike's title card: "they've got the cracked screen the digital cracked screen down the middle where it's like breaking You're visually hinting at the entire idea". [D] same file.
- The fix he gives Jairo Obando on Qdoba: "you need some sort of key visual to communicate what the entire campaign is about and right now the Billboards and out of home stuff feels like extensions of the idea". [D] `video/art-director-reacts-to-portfolios-live.md`
- The same goes for the thumbnail. Of Michael Orfanos's GWM billboards he says: "I never actually got that from the thumbnail". [D] same file.

### Step 11. Build the project page

His Project Checklist, seven lines, read off the FigJam slide at 9 min 36 s. [D:vis] `frames/how-to-craft-an-art-director-portfolio.md`

1. Brand
2. Big Idea Title/Name
3. Keyvisual or Lockup (optional)
4. Overview/Description
5. Insight + Idea
6. 4-6+ touchpoints/executions
7. "Between touchpoints, include some of your thinking. How it all works together."

The wireframe beside it runs top to bottom: Key Visual or Lockup, Brand + Idea Title, Hero Executions (three boxes), Execution Cont'd. On the insight and idea he says: "you need to prove that there is an insight and idea there whether you label it overtly or not". [D] `video/how-to-craft-an-art-director-portfolio.md`

### Step 12. If it can be done for real, do it

- His own student stunt, Venmo the Homeless: "we raised $6,000 for that homeless shelter the Food and Care Coalition". [D] `video/how-to-pivot-to-art-direction-with-no-experience.md`
- His table row for Stunt / Cultural Response: "Ideally do something for real that garners attention." [D:vis] `frames/how-to-craft-an-art-director-portfolio.md`
- In June 2026 he shot his own Oakley spec with photographer friends and mocked it up in place. [D] `shorts/2026-06-08-don-t-wait-to-creative-direct-brands-fill-your-portfolio-wit-GPK3d7-GQgA.md`

### Step 13. Write it up with STAR

- "the formula I'll share is called star it's an acronym it's situation s situation T task a actions and then R results". [D] `video/art-director-reacts-to-portfolios-live.md`
- STAR is for the case-study write-up of a finished project. NotebookLM presents it as a way to write a brief, which he never says. [M]

## Insight examples: insight, idea, execution

### Snickers, "You're Not You When You're Hungry"

- Insight: "Hunger doesn't just affect energy. It temporarily changes how people behave and how they treat others. It makes them act out of character." [D:vis] `frames/how-to-become-an-art-director-in-2026.md`
- Idea: "Show that Snickers can snap people back to being themselves when hunger turns them into someone they're not." [D:vis] same file.
- Execution: people act wildly out of character, shown as exaggerated versions of someone else, until they eat a Snickers. In speech he names Betty White. [D] `video/how-to-become-an-art-director-in-2025.md`
- The 2025 board had a fifth line on top, the observation: "People get irritable when they're hungry". [D:vis] `frames/how-to-pivot-to-art-direction-with-no-experience.md`

### Dove, "Real Beauty"

- Insight: "Most women judge their appearance far more harshly than others do." [D:vis] `frames/how-to-become-an-art-director-in-2026.md`
- Idea: "Show women how others actually see them, so they can give themselves permission to see their own beauty." [D:vis] same file.
- Execution: "Real women, minimal retouching, and emotional experiments like the forensic sketch artist reveal the gap between self-judgment and outside perception". [D:vis] same file.
- Record [G]: his panel folds two campaigns into one. Campaign for Real Beauty is 2004, Ogilvy & Mather. The sketch artist film is Real Beauty Sketches, 2013, Ogilvy Brasil. `mentioned/campaigns/dove-campaign-for-real-beauty.md`

### Spotify, "Wrapped"

- Insight: "People use music taste as a way to define themselves and signal who they are." [D:vis] `frames/how-to-become-an-art-director-in-2026.md`
- Idea: "Turn personal listening behavior into a story people recognize as themselves and want to share." [D:vis] same file.

### Nike, "Winning Isn't for Everyone"

- Insight: "most people like the idea of winning but very few are willing to accept the sacrifice the obsession the unlikability it often requires winning means not always being the hero". [D] `video/how-to-become-an-art-director-in-2026.md`

### Nike, "Rip the Script"

- Insight: "football is increasingly feeling more planned and strategic, maybe a little staged. But, the most beautiful moments are when players follow their instincts." [D] `shorts/2026-06-16-how-creative-directors-create-big-ideas-MhMhug5xo9E.md`

- Execution: a hero film, athlete vignettes, and content that only borrows the look. Ingredients he lists: "bts hollywood sets, cracked screens, SMPTE color bars, and digital glitching". [D] `newsletter/2026-06-23-the-most-feral-art-direction-in-sports-right-now-mad-007.md`

### Arc'teryx, "Playdate" (student spec, Connor McLoughlin)

- Insight: "As we age, we experience more bad weather, but become worse at handling it. The more we avoid discomfort, the more sensitive to it we become." [D:vis] `frames/art-direction-techniques-you-can-use.md`

### EF Tours, "Later Is Now" (student spec, Jaya Schreihart)

### Santo Domingo (Mark Pollard's exercise)

- This one is Pollard's, shown on Jason's screen. [X:Mark Pollard], [D:vis] `frames/how-to-pivot-to-art-direction-with-no-experience.md`

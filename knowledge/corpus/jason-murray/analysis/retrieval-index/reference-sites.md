## FILMGRAB

Also written: Film Grab, film-grab.com, FilmGrab.

- **What it is:** A free archive of hand-picked film stills, one gallery per film. It started in 2010 and one cinematographer runs it. [G]
- **How it slices visual work:** By film title first (Films A-Z, 4,157 titles). By maker: Directors, Cinematographers, Production Designers and Costume Designers, each A-Z. By Aspect Ratio (21 values), Country (95), Genre (25: Horror, Comedy, Crime, Sci Fi, Action, Thriller, Biography, Romance, Fantasy, Based on a Book, Musical, Western, Comic, War, New Wave, LGBTQ, Animation, Drama, Documentary, Martial Arts, Sports, Road Movie, Based on a Play, High School, Silent) and Year by decade. Single stills carry no tags, so there is no shot type, lighting, colour or mood filter. [G]
- **What he says:** [D] "film grab is one of my favorite sources of movie Stills it's simple and free" (`shorts/2024-11-11-is-there-a-pinterest-for-film-_QQihNL1mE0.md`). [D] April 2026 livestream: "it's free cuz there's ads", "You can browse by artist", and "the only reason why uh why I'll go to some of the other ones is just search" (`video/visuals-you-can-t-find-on-pinterest.md`). [D] July 3, 2026: "When someone first shared Film Grab with me it was so hush hush like “Shhhh don’t show the other art directors this secret site where you can get sick visuals”" (`linkedin/014-2026-07-03.md`). [D] Toolbox line: "A library of high quality movie stills for keyframes, mood boards and general deck vibes." (`toolbox/tools.json`)
- **How he uses it:** Keyframes, mood boards and decks, per his Toolbox line. In the livestream he scrolls it for stills from real films and opens Steven Soderbergh's Black Bag. [D] [D:vis]
- **Free or paid:** Free, by his words. The scraped file adds that Patreon and a consultancy page fund it. [G]
- **URL:** https://film-grab.com

Composition: Center, Left heavy, Right heavy, Balanced, Symmetrical, Short side. Lens Size: Ultra Wide / Fisheye, Wide, Medium, Long, Telephoto. Lighting Type: Daylight, Sunny, Overcast, Moonlight, Artificial light, Practical light, Firelight, Fluorescent, Tungsten, HMI, LED, Mixed light. Each still also has a "Similar Shots" button. ## Cosmos

Also written: cosmos.so, "Kosmos" in captions.

- **What it is:** A saving and discovery app for designers. You save images, videos, links, notes and products as "elements" and group them into collections. [G]
- **How it slices visual work:** Explore has 17 categories: Fashion, Interiors, Graphic Design, Art, Branding, Typography, Architecture, Weddings, Interfaces, Nature, Cinema, Motion, Technology, Portraiture, Quotes, Emotion, Symbol & Myth, each with about 20 sub-topics. Sub-topics mix makers (Paul Rand, Wong Kar-wai), styles (Brutalism, Film Noir), techniques (Risograph, Stop Motion, Tintype) and formats (Poster, Title Design). Emotion holds Calm, Desire, Euphoria, Frenzy, Intimacy, Joy, Longing, Melancholy, Nostalgia, Rage, Serenity, Solitude. Search by keyword, by colour (picker or hex code), by image, or a mix.

## Eyecandy overlap

Eyecandy (eyecannndy.com) sorts film clips under 136 technique names. Jason shows it for about 8 seconds in the Short "Is there a Pinterest for film?" and it is the closest public site to Iris. [D:vis] `frames/shorts-visual-tools.md`. The category list is in `mentioned/tools/eyecandy/techniques.json`, [X:Eyecandy].

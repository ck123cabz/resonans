# The Toolbox: every tool, by category

Jason Murray's public bookmark database at thetoolbox.art, scraped 2026-10-03. It holds 129 tools. Each H2 below is one of his category tags, with the tab name the site shows. Every description is his own line from the Description column, copied word for word. Provenance for every table row: [D] `toolbox/tools.json` (same rows in `toolbox/tools.md`). A tool with two tags appears under both, so each section stands alone. "Added" is the date he created the row in Notion.

Source paths are relative to `knowledge/corpus/jason-murray/.raw/`.

## What The Toolbox is

- **Type:** [RES]
- **Provenance:** [D] "The Toolbox is a community, database, newsletter & podcast sharing creative tools and resources for art directors, designers, writers and creators." (`blog/toolbox-about.md`)
- **URL:** https://thetoolbox.art
- **Price:** free. The about page says the site carries affiliate links and that commissions cover hosting.
- **Tagline:** [D] "It's time to unleash all the secrets!" (`blog/toolbox-about.md`)
- **How it is built:** [D] "I turned it into a website that's connected to my own notion database where I can just upload the the best resources that I actually use" (`video/why-i-m-starting-a-live-podcast-for-creatives-the-toolbox-podcast-ep-1.md`)
- **Submitting a tool:** the Submit page embeds a NoteForms form, "share a creative tool" (`blog/toolbox-submit.md`).
- **Where it sits in his business:** [D] "It's not the main money maker. It's primarily a free resource for the industry." (`shorts/2025-05-06-how-i-make-money-as-an-independent-creative-Jguhxc8z0Fs.md`). The Toolbox brand also covers the newsletter, the Instagram account and the live podcast. See `career-and-business.md`.
- **Paid versions:** The Toolbox Database, the same list as a Notion database you can duplicate, $10 on Gumroad. The Toolbox OS, a Notion dashboard, $40 regular price. See `tools-and-gear.md`.

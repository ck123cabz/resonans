# Tools and gear: what Jason Murray uses, sells and says about each

One H3 per item: what he uses it for and his verdict. Source paths are relative to `knowledge/corpus/jason-murray/.raw/`. Markers: `[D]` his words, `[D:vis]` read off a frame, `[D:guest:<name>]` a guest's words, `[G]` a price or fact from a product page, `[M]` NotebookLM wording with no source found, `[I]` inferred, `[A]` a gap.

Not in this file: reference sites and archives are in `reference-sites.md`. The 129 Toolbox bookmarks, most of which he lists with one line and never talks about, are in `toolbox.md`.

Sponsor flags matter here. Framer, Musicbed, Adobe, Wix Studio, Penji, ManyChat, Pacdora, Microsoft and Shopify have all paid him or partnered with him. Each entry says so.

## Procreate and Procreate Dreams techniques

Background layer. These come from his 2020 to 2023 tutorials. One H3 per technique.

### Handwriting reveal by erasing backwards (Procreate)

- **Provenance:** [D] "all i did was use the crystals and the push liquify effects to get the kind of explosion effect that i wanted" (`video/animated-handwriting-signature-or-hand-drawn-logo-effect-procreate-tut.md`)
- **Steps:** 1. White on black, Studio Pen. 2. Turn on Animation Assist at 12 frames per second. 3. Draw the full signature. 4. Duplicate the frame and erase a little from the end. Repeat. The gap between erase points sets the speed. 5. Optional: an intro dot, and an explosion exit made with the Crystals and Push liquify effects.

### Handwriting reveal with a layer mask and colour (Procreate Dreams)

- **Provenance:** [D] "select all of those frames group them and then turn that group into a layer mask" (`video/handwriting-animation-in-procreate-dreams-w-color-effect.md`)
- **Steps:** 1. Put the logo on a track. 2. On a track above, trace the strokes frame by frame. 3. Select the frames, group them, turn the group into a layer mask. 4. Duplicate underneath in a bright colour and offset by a frame or two.
- **File:** free on Gumroad.

### Liquid logo with a clipping mask (Procreate)

- **Provenance:** [M] steps not checked (`video/liquid-apple-logo-animation-procreate-tutorial.md`)
- **Steps:** 1. Logo as the guide layer. 2. A clipping-mask layer for the liquid. 3. A drips layer. 4. Draw frame by frame at 12 frames per second.

### Liquid logo the easy way (Procreate)

- **Provenance:** [M] steps not checked (`video/liquid-apple-logo-animation-the-easy-way-procreate-tutorial.md`)
- **Steps:** 1. Erase wavy sections frame by frame until the logo is gone. 2. Reverse the frame order so it reads as a fill. 3. Play at 6 frames per second.

### Glitch on a logo (Procreate 5X)

- **Provenance:** [D] "signal now this one i've been really enjoying it really distorts like the positioning of whatever you're affecting" (`video/easy-glitch-animation-effects-in-procreate-5x.md`)
- **Steps:** 1. Duplicate the logo across frames. 2. Apply a glitch effect and raise the amount frame by frame. 3. Lower it again. His pick is Signal; Chromatic Aberration and Diverge are confirmed by name.

### Animating on a product photo (Procreate)

- **Provenance:** [D] "let's drop the frames down to six frames per second because we're going to keep the motion a little bit more gif like" (`video/how-to-animate-on-product-photos-in-procreate.md`)
- **Steps:** 1. Set the photo as the background frame. 2. Draw over it frame by frame. 3. Play at 6 frames per second.
- **Styles NotebookLM lists:** a swooping line behind the product, a squiggly outline, a liquid fill. [M] not checked.

### Reusable logo animation template (Procreate Dreams)

- **Provenance:** [D] "right away we're going to overshoot the size a little bit bit that way it'll have a bit of a bounce" (`video/make-your-own-logo-animation-template-in-procreate-dreams.md`)
- **Steps:** 1. Put the logo in a group. 2. Keyframe the group: cut in small, overshoot, settle. 3. Add hand-drawn pop lines with the Monoline brush, grouped and rotated. 4. Swap the logo inside the group to reuse it.
- **File:** the 'Pop' template, free on Gumroad.

### Cut-out puppet animation (Procreate Dreams)

- **Provenance:** [D] "it's kind of like paper cutout puppets we're gluing certain pieces together but some pieces we know we want to make them move" (`video/nickelodeon-logo-animation-in-procreate-dreams-8-different-styles.md`)
- **Steps:** 1. Get layered PSDs from an illustrator. 2. Group the parts that stay together. 3. Move the loose parts.
- **His rule:** [D] "the illustration and the design often carries a lot of the weight and does a lot of the heavy lifting for animations motion design". Hire the illustrator.
- **Note:** the title says eight styles. He names seven.

### Transparent export for LumaFusion

- **Provenance:** [D] "it's an animated HEVC file and when you click that you get the option to export with a transparent background" (`video/how-to-export-animations-with-transparent-background-for-lumafusion-pr.md`)
- **Steps:** 1. Turn off the background layer. 2. Export Animated HEVC. 3. Tick transparent background. 4. Lay it over footage in LumaFusion, "a cheap app for less than 30 dollars".

### Nomad Sculpt to Procreate 3D painting

- **Provenance:** [D] "first we just need to uv unwrap each of the parts of the model" (`video/easy-procreate-3d-model-spider-man-nomadsculpt-workflow.md`)
- **Steps:** 1. Block the model from primitives in Nomad Sculpt. 2. UV unwrap each part. 3. Export OBJ. 4. Import and paint in Procreate. Procreate has no 3D symmetry, so mirrored lines are drawn twice.

### Drawing callouts over footage (the MKBHD remake)

- **Provenance:** [D] "I used a little bit of putty to stick the calculator to my little camera arm that attaches to my desk" (`video/i-recreated-mkbhd-s-iphone-15-pro-animation-shot-for-shot.md`)
- **Steps:** 1. Shoot the object on a desk arm. 2. Bring the footage into Procreate. 3. Draw white callout lines on every frame.

### Frame rates

- **Provenance:** [D] "it actually makes the imperfections and the Jitter of your drawings look a little smoother it has the opposite effect on film footage so that's why 24 and 30 frames per second is more ideal" (same video)
- **Rule:** 6 or 12 frames per second for hand-drawn work. 24 or 30 over footage.

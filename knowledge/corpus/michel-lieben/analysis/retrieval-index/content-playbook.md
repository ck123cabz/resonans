## LinkedIn Posting Rules

### Content Principles
1. **Highly practical, actionable advice.** Solves an immediate problem. Document day-to-day work: campaigns that performed, tools tested, workarounds discovered.
2. **Go as deep as possible.** Hit character limits. Fewer people who find you great is better than many who find you "just good."
3. **Write everything personally.** Michel writes 100% of his content. Spends ~3 hours on weekends. Rejects fully autonomous AI content generation.
4. **Use AI as a drafting assistant.** Claude Code (Opus) via Conductor for hook variations, content repurposing, brainstorming angles. 15-20 minutes from idea to final draft.

### Visual Strategy
- Custom infographics/schemas increase impressions 3-10x
- Visual takes up entire mobile screen = second hook
- In-house design team (Ada, Pilar, Maria Luisa) produces all graphics
- Carousels outperform every other format (more saves, shares, profile visits)
- Canva for beginners; Figma/Adobe for production

### Algorithmic Tactics
- Post Monday through Friday, every day
- Comment on other creators' posts 30 minutes before publishing
- Reply to every comment within the first 30 minutes
- GIFs outperform equivalent short video posts (unclear why, treat as reliable pattern)

### Repurposing
- Take best-performing post, publish again every couple months
- Michel's tech stack infographic published 5+ times at 100K+ impressions each
- Most viewers forget posts. New followers never saw them.

### What Not to Do
- Don't use AI to scrape news and write 100 posts in 15 minutes (gets zero engagement)
- Don't spread across too many platforms (master one, dominate it, then add complementary formats)
- Don't post low-effort content just to hit numbers (quality > frequency)

## Interactive SEO Strategy

**Concept:** Embed functional mini-tools directly inside blog articles. Reader can experience the value instantly without signing up or talking to sales.

**Primary example:** Email/phone finding tool. Reader lands on ColdIQ tutorial about finding contact information. Types their first name, last name, and company website into the embedded tool. Tool finds their email address in real time on the page.

**Why it works:**
- Frictionless proof of competence (no signup required)
- Instant trust (methodology works on the reader themselves)
- Higher conversions to affiliate links, community signups, or agency calls
- AI chatbots cannot replicate the interactive experience

**Implementation tools:** Claude Code for building mini-tools, Lovable and Vercel for deployment, Webflow for hosting.

**Mini-tools built by ColdIQ (embedded in blog articles):**
- Lookalike Finder (enter company URL, find similar organizations)
- Intent Signals (check which companies show buying signals in your space)
- People Finder (find decision-makers at target accounts)
- Email Finder (find verified email addresses)
- Campaign Ideation (generate campaign angles based on ICP)
- LinkedIn Post Previewer (preview content before publishing)
- GTM Report (personalized GTM strategy report)

## Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)

Michel believes traditional SEO (write tutorial, insert affiliate links) is becoming obsolete as users ask AI chatbots instead of clicking search results.

**Strategy:** Create content not just for humans but for LLMs to consume. Ensure ColdIQ is referenced as an authoritative source when AI chatbots generate answers.

**Complementary moves:**

- "G2 for sales" directory with expert video tutorials instead of biased text reviews

## LinkedIn Ads Funnel

Michel's LinkedIn Ads are not standalone. They amplify organic content that already proved it can book meetings.

### Three-Layer Structure

**Top of Funnel (Build Trust):**
- Document Ads: 5-8 page carousels, pure tactical value. 2-3x engagement vs. single image ads.
- Single Image Ads: pain/fear hook + solution. One message per image.
- Thought-Leader Mini-Demo Videos: 15-30 seconds, founder showing something real. Authenticity is the point.

**Middle of Funnel (Bring Proof):**
- Retargeting from 90-day engagement window (website visitors, post engagers, company page followers)

**Bottom of Funnel (Convert):**
- Direct response to hot audiences

### Process
1. Review which organic posts booked meetings
2. Identify which persona booked from those posts
3. Boost the post in front of the same persona

### Results
- $5,100 spent in July 2025 = 155K extra impressions at $33 CPM
- $80K+ ARR added from LinkedIn Ads in 2025

**Warning:** Broad Google Ads without strict parameters waste budget. Algorithm bids on your own brand name, paying for clicks you would have won organically. Ad platforms always claim credit for conversions even when organic content did the heavy lifting.

# AI Prompt Library

Every prompt, prompt structure and prompting rule Erhart teaches, at his wording. Source: `q32-ai-prompting.md`. Provenance `[S]`.

---

## The six rules

1. **Assign a role before asking anything.** "You are a world-class marketing expert." "You are my market research assistant." "Act as a conversion rate expert, direct response copywriter, and web designer all at once."
2. **Give deep context and hard constraints.** Business type, audience, goals, current traffic sources, specific pain points. Then output limits: word count, tone ("confident but conversational", "warm, friendly, candid"), structure ("include a hook, 3 key insights, and a call to action").
3. **Feed a draft or real data first.** Do not ask for a blank-page write. Paste your rough draft, your customer survey transcripts, your homepage copy, then ask it to polish, clarify or extract. *This is the rule that separates his prompting from generic advice: AI compresses and polishes existing material rather than originating it.*
4. **Treat output one as 80% and refine with targeted commands.** "Make it shorter." "Make it funnier." "Make it less cheesy." "Remove emojis." "Rewrite using the PAS framework."
5. **Persona emulation works.** "Write like Adam Erhart" (spelled E-R-H-A-R-T). It pulls from a decade of published content.
6. **Keep a human in the loop.** AI supplies speed and volume; the operator supplies strategy, filtering and brand alignment. The Formula 1 analogy: AI is the car, you are the driver.

## Market research

**Target market discovery**
> "I need to generate leads for my [type of business], but I can't figure out who my target market is. Please give me a few different target markets to test and make sure to highlight why you chose them."

**Miracles and Miseries scraper**
> "You are my market research assistant. I'm going to be launching a new marketing campaign targeting [niche, e.g. doggy daycare owners] and I need you to tell me as much information as you possibly can about their biggest pains, problems, fears, and frustrations, as well as their wants, needs, dreams, goals, and desires, and their average revenue and employee structure."

**Survey summarisation**
> "Summarize this [X]-page customer survey into five key takeaways for my marketing team. Focus on customer pain points, buying behavior, and trends."
Then paste the survey text.

**Competitor list**
> "Please list out five competitors in the [market/industry] and include their websites."

**Keywords**
> "Please give me a list of 10 to 20 different keywords that are relevant to a [description of brand/offer]."

> "Give me 10 longtail keywords related to [topic]. Prioritize keywords with high search volume but low competition."

**Behavioural pattern analysis**
> "Analyze this client data and identify what behavioral patterns distinguish the [X] people who became clients from the [Y] who didn't. What early signals predict long-term clients versus quick churn?"

## Ad copy

**Core formula**
> "Write me a [kind of text, e.g. Facebook ad] that is [style and tone, e.g. persuasive, funny, and conversational], highlighting/featuring [characteristics, e.g. the benefits and importance of having a solid marketing strategy]."

**Targeted niche offer**
> "Please create a Facebook ad that targets [target market, e.g. homeowners] with our [offer, e.g. 24-hour plumbing service]."

**PAS framework**
> "Write me a Facebook ad for [offer] using the PAS (Problem, Agitate, Solve) copywriting framework. Lead with a strong hook that calls out [target market]."

**Page to ad**
Paste the sales page or homepage copy, then:
> "Write me a Facebook ad between 150 and 200 words based on this offer."

**The short optimiser**
> "Improve clarity and persuasion."

Extended:
> "Improve clarity and persuasion. Make it targeted, unique, credible, and action-driven."

## Email and outreach

**Cold email polish**
> "Take this cold email and improve it to be more persuasive and conversion focused. Keep it concise, make the call to action stronger, and ensure it sounds natural and engaging. [Insert draft email here]"

**Psychology-driven email** (note the built-in interview step)
> "Write me a high-converting email in the style of Adam Erhart using proven marketing psychology. Keep it conversational, story-driven, and designed to educate, build trust, and lead naturally into my offer. Before writing this email, ask me two to three specific questions to understand my business, my offer, and my target market."

**Motivation-split subject lines**
> "I'm selling [product/service]. My audience includes people motivated by [value 1], [value 2], and [value 3]. Write three versions of this email subject line, each appealing to one of these specific motivations. [Insert current subject line]"

**Cold DM generator**
> "I'm going to focus exclusively on direct outreach using cold DMs on Facebook, Instagram, and LinkedIn. Can you please suggest three different cold DM scripts ranging from 50 to 150 words?"

## Operations

**SOP**
> "Create a step-by-step standard operating procedure (SOP) for [process, e.g. running a monthly email marketing campaign]. Include timeline, tasks, and key performance metrics."

**Job description**
> "Write a job description for a [role, e.g. performance marketing specialist focused on Facebook and Google ads]. Include responsibilities, required skills, and preferred experience level."

**Business plan**
> "Write a complete business plan for [business idea]. Include target market, pricing, competitive analysis of [list of competitor websites], and launch strategy."

**Clarity coaching**
> "I feel overwhelmed with my marketing strategy. Coach me through a five-step clarity exercise to help me prioritize tasks and take action."

## Templates and positioning

**Fill-in-the-blank page template**
> "Create a fill-in-the-blank template for a high-converting webinar landing page [or sales page / email sequence]. Include a compelling headline, bullet points for benefits, and a strong CTA."

**Clear Offer Formula value proposition**
> "Help me write a clean, clear, concise, short value proposition using the following framework: We help [Niche] business owners get [Result you deliver] using [Unique Mechanism] so they can [Things they want] without [Things they hate / don't want]."

## The six-prompt campaign chain

One session, sequential, each step inheriting the previous step's positioning so the campaign stays coherent. The value is the ordering, not the individual prompts.

1. **Ad**, the core ad copy formula above.
2. **Sales page**, "Write a complete sales page for [offer] based on the Facebook ad written above." Then ask it to expand key sections with a 500-word blog-style breakdown of the core benefit.
3. **Social**, "Turn that blog post/sales page into three shorter social media posts for Instagram and LinkedIn."
4. **Email**, "Write a 3-part email welcome and nurture sequence based on this offer."
5. **SMS**, "Turn that email sequence into a 3-part SMS text message sequence. Make it short and conversational."
6. **Translation**, "Translate this entire campaign into [language]."

## Content and social

**Repurposing**
> "Give me three high-performing Instagram posts, a LinkedIn carousel outline, and a YouTube short script based on this [paste top-performing blog post or video transcript]."

**Caption generator**
> "Please generate three different captions, one short, one medium, and one long, to be used as a social media post alongside a picture of [description of image]."

**The "ghost" local business post generator** (the free service that becomes a $500/mo offer)
> "Create a professional Facebook feed post for [Business Name], a [Business Type] in [City]. Highlight [Specific Detail/Offer]. Target local customers. Generate: 1) a high-quality realistic image in a modern, clean, candid lifestyle style that would appeal to a broad audience, 2) a caption under 50 words in a warm, friendly, conversational, helpful tone, 3) include a subtle call to action such as learn more, book now, visit today, or stop by. Make it feel authentic, on-brand, and like a real local business post, not generic AI content."

## Image generation

**Photorealism modifiers.** Add "hyper realistic", "ultra high def photo", "candid lifestyle style", "modern clean" to avoid a cartoonish stock look.

**The 5 visual angles**
> "Please give me five examples for visuals to use alongside this ad: 1) literal, 2) metaphorical, 3) dream outcome, 4) funny, and 5) curiosity-provoking."

**Headline to image**
> "Design an image that fits the tone of this headline and matches my brand colors."

## The 5-minute AI Studio website prompt

Upload screenshots of a local business's current site and their Google reviews, then:

> "Act as a conversion rate expert, a direct response copywriter, and a web designer all at once. Analyze everything, their branding, current page structure, what's working, and what's killing their conversions. Then write a complete, ready-to-paste AI Studio prompt based on this specific business using their exact brand colors, actual customer reviews, and actual services."

A prompt that writes the prompt. This is the mechanism behind the free-website outreach play in `scripts-library.md`.

# Tool Stack -- Kole Jain

**Corpus:** kole-jain
**Generated:** 2026-07-22
**Provenance:** [S] throughout -- single-source NotebookLM corpus (signals S129-S134, S61, S118)

Categorized inventory of every tool, plugin, and resource Kole recommends, with what he actually uses each for. A good chunk comes from his "20 Underrated Web Design Resources for 2025" list, which actually contains 21 items despite the title.

---

## Research & inspiration

- **Mobin:** his most-cited tool by a wide margin. Used to research thousands of real-world design patterns, full user flows (onboarding, checkout), and specific components (charts, CTAs) from top shipped apps. The backbone of his research step before any design work.
- **Unsectioned:** searchable sections pulled from live websites; he uses it for layout variety when his own sections start looking samey.
- **Wall of Portfolios:** portfolio showcase broken down by designer experience level and location, recommended for calibrating your own expectations against real peers.
- **Dribbble / Pinterest / Behance / Instagram:** general inspiration gathering for portfolio projects and swipe files. His 28-day plan has beginners collecting ~20 hero and feature sections from Mobin, Dribbble, or Pinterest before ever opening Figma. He does say a personal site beats Dribbble/Behance as the actual portfolio host.

## Figma plugins

- **HTML to Design:** imports live websites or Claude-generated code as fully editable Figma layers. The bridge in his LLM-to-Figma workflow (S118).
- **Mobin plugin:** pulls real-world UI elements and patterns from Mobin's library without leaving Figma.
- **Noise & Texture:** adds noise to gradients and text for the "premium" feel he keeps coming back to.
- **To Path:** circular and curved text, mostly for stickers and UI badges.
- **Phosphor Icons and Feather Icons:** his go-to libraries for clean, minimal interface icons.
- **Flat Icon:** for hunting down a specific interface icon and downloading it as an SVG.

## AI generation

- **Claude:** the core engine of his "vibe designing" workflow. Generates HTML/CSS for complex components (dashboards, Gantt charts) that would be slow to build by hand; he rates it specifically for UI component code.
- **ChatGPT:** writes project briefs for fake portfolio projects and punchy natural-language landing page copy. Copy and briefs, not code.
- **21st.dev:** library of UI components built with or for AI. He copies exact code or prompt snippets from it straight into Claude.
- **Reloom:** AI site builder; the real draw for him is the bundled 54-page Figma design kit.

## Motion & animation

- **Phase:** animation platform for Figma designs, which he finds more intuitive than After Effects.
- **LottieFiles:** animation library; he points specifically at the playlists curated by professional designers.
- **Design Spells:** curated micro-interactions collected from billion-dollar companies, used as motion reference.

## 3D

- **Spline:** 3D animated assets (spinning logos and the like) without touching code.
- **Blender:** same job as Spline, still no-code from the design side.
- **Endless Tools:** customizable 3D resources and assets.

## Design-to-code

- **Matter (by JetBrains):** prototypes Figma designs into live, sharable previews for web and native Mac apps. Also one of his rapid-scaffolding options from Figma links or screenshots; AI generates the skeleton, the GSAP motion work stays manual (S61).
- **Builder.io:** AI front-end engineer that understands existing codebases and design systems, used for visual iteration directly in the codebase, Figma to production.
- **Dualite Alpha:** turns a Figma link into a functional web app in under two minutes; his fast path to making a portfolio piece clickable.

## Color

- **oklch.com:** perception-based color picker, used to keep brightness consistent across different hues.
- **Tailwind CSS palette:** used as a color reference, not a framework dependency. He grabs a hue's "50" value for backgrounds to get professional near-whites.

## Assets & fonts

- **Shopify Burst:** Shopify's free stock photo site; he rates it for how well it's organized.
- **Photo Gradient:** presets and settings for mesh gradients.
- **Typefaces.today:** unique, high-quality display fonts.
- **Fonty:** maps fonts on a 3D plane to generate custom pairings.
- **Isocons:** customizable isometric icons.
- **Logo Ipsum:** placeholder logos and animations for people who, his words, suck at logos.
- **Logo System:** library of logos and interesting backgrounds for inspiration.
- **Logo.com:** logo generation and ideas.
- **Derves:** niche slider assets he suggests for crypto and modern SaaS landing pages.
- **Nukes:** trending and top designs "ripped straight off of Twitter."
- **Handy Arrows:** a wide variety of arrow styles.
- **UIverse.io:** buttons, animations, and elements that copy directly into Figma. This is the 21st item that makes the "20" title wrong.

## His own free Figma files

He ships a free Figma file with nearly every tutorial and redesign so beginners can learn by cloning. The named ones:

- **Reloom Design Kit:** 54 pages of layouts and sections.
- **11 Micro Animations:** sleek interactions and satisfying hover effects.
- **Unicorn AI Startup Components:** seven key components for AI interface design.
- **7 Modern UI Layouts:** patterns extracted from 50 top software companies.
- **Redesign project files:** full files for his Google Gemini, Spotify Wrapped, and Fisker ("Tesla Wannabe") redesigns.

## Career

- **Final Round AI:** AI co-pilot that automates job applications and gives real-time interview support.
- **Design Sphere:** directory of web design podcasts; his pick from it is Honest UX Talks.
- **UX of AI:** site for getting a feel for designing in AI-adjacent fields, which is where he thinks the career leverage is heading.

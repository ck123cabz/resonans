# Operations: Offer Creation -- Christian Bonnier Retrieval Index

The Copywriter Agent uses this file when constructing or diagnosing an offer before writing copy. Offer quality is upstream of copy quality. If the offer is wrong, no amount of copy craft will save the campaign.

---

## XYZ Framework

> "We help [X: Audience] accomplish [Y: Quantifiable Benefit] with [Z: Unique Mechanism]."
> -- NLM Signal 8, confirmed `[D]`

**Components:**
- **X (Audience):** Specific. Not "businesses" but "ecommerce brands doing $5M+ in revenue."
- **Y (Quantifiable Benefit):** Tangible, specific. "15 sales calls" or "50 free leads" -- not "marketing" or "ROAS."
- **Z (Unique Mechanism):** How you deliver the result. This is where differentiation lives.

**Constraint: Offer Anchoring.** Anchor to a physical/tangible deliverable or hard numerical outcome the prospect can visualize. Strip away marketing jargon. Pitch tangible end-results with hyper-specific industry slang. (NLM Signal 8, 22)

## Intangible + Concrete + Proof Layering

LinkedIn Signal 4 expands the XYZ Framework into a persuasion stack:

| Layer | Function | Example |
|-------|----------|---------|
| Layer 1: Intangible benefit | Creates emotion | "make you go viral on TikTok" |
| Layer 2: Concrete guarantee | Removes risk | "1 million views in 90 days or we work for free" |
| Layer 3: Proof | Backs up claims | "Fragile Collective got 50K in sales from organic content" |

> "Only emotional benefit = hype. Only guarantee = transactional. All three together = believable promise."
> -- LinkedIn Signal 4 `[V]`

**Mapping to XYZ:** Y (Quantifiable Benefit) maps to Layer 2 (concrete guarantee). Z (Unique Mechanism) maps to Layer 1 (intangible benefit). LinkedIn adds Layer 3 (proof) as an explicit component not in the NLM formulation.

## Market Sophistication Framework (Offer Levels)

Before writing any copy, diagnose the market. The market position determines copy strategy.

### NLM Three-Level Model

| Level | Market State | Copy Strategy | Source |
|-------|-------------|---------------|--------|
| Level 1 | High Demand (prospects actively seeking) | Pitch directly. Lead with the service itself. | NLM Signal 1 |
| Level 2 | Latent Demand / High Saturation | Lead with guarantees and risk reversals. | NLM Signal 1 |
| Level 3 | No Perceived Demand | Hide pitch behind free value or lead magnet. | NLM Signal 1 |

**Level 1 examples:** Lead gen, podcast placements, commission-only closers.
**Level 2 examples:** Email marketing, SMM, paid ads.
**Level 3 examples:** Web design, SEO, Facebook ads.

### LinkedIn Four-Tier Expansion

| Market Type | Lead With | Source |
|------------|----------|--------|
| New market (TikTok strategy) | Innovation. Just mentioning the channel gets attention. | LinkedIn Signal 45 |
| Saturated market (Facebook ads) | Results, case studies, specific revenue numbers, risk reversals. | LinkedIn Signal 45 |
| Mature market | Differentiation. What makes you different from every other provider? | LinkedIn Signal 45 |
| Emerging market | Education. Teach them why they need this. | LinkedIn Signal 45 |

> "This is why copying 'winning templates' is killing your campaigns."

## Offer Angle Invention

When your service is commoditized, invent a proprietary angle.

**Process (LinkedIn Signal 8, NLM Signal 59):**
1. Break down every component of your service
2. Identify which components have never been pitched over cold email
3. That becomes your new angle

**Example:** Ecom email marketing agency breaks down into customer interviews, Reddit research, copywriting, flow optimization, deliverability, design. Instead of pitching "15% revenue boost," pitch: "We interview 100 of your customers and use their language in your email flows." (LinkedIn Signal 8)

> "You don't need to have fulfilled this before. If the market wants to buy it, pitch it and figure out the fulfillment once you have leads coming in."
> -- LinkedIn Signal 8 `[V]`

## Features vs. Benefits (Nuanced)

The standard "always lead with benefits" advice has exceptions.

| Offer Type | Lead With | Reasoning | Source |
|-----------|----------|-----------|--------|
| Trendy/high-demand (TikTok ads) | Feature itself | Market actively wants this mechanism | NLM Signal 11 |
| Saturated (Facebook ads) | Benefits | Feature is commoditized | NLM Signal 11 |
| Broad/full-service | Individual sub-features | A/B test which specific angle the market responds to; sell full stack on the phone | NLM Signal 11 |

This maps to the Offer Levels: Level 1 can lead with features, Level 2/3 must lead with benefits or value.

## Pricing Strategies

### The Setup/Asset Model

Charge a one-time setup fee (e.g., $1,500) where the client owns the built asset outright. Bypasses the standard $2K/month retainer model. (NLM Signal 9)

### The Paid Scoping Sprint

For $100K-$200K deals, sell a $5K-$10K scoping sprint first. Get paid to investigate pain points for a few weeks. Effectively getting paid to sell the six-figure backend. Proven in the Dallas case study. (NLM Signal 9, 46)

### Never State Pricing in Cold Email

> "Avoid stating prices over cold email... Say this: 'Our pricing is flexible based on your needs - the best next step is to jump on a quick call so we can offer you the best package for your situation'"
> -- X Signal 6 `[V]`

**Exception:** If your price IS your proprietary advantage (e.g., one-time $1,500 fee replacing a retainer), stating it can be the differentiator. (NLM Signal 9)

## A/B Angle Testing Methodology

**Process (LinkedIn Signals 5-6):**
1. List every benefit of your service
2. Split benefits into emotional vs. logical categories
3. Write one offer angle per benefit
4. Test 4 angles head-to-head (or up to 8 if volume allows)
5. Double down on the winner

**Case study:** Short-term rental cleaning. Tested 4 angles:
- Emotional: "clean your property without lifting a finger"
- Emotional: "leave a perfect first impression"
- Logical: "collect more 5-star reviews"
- Logical: "generate more income"

"Generate more income" won by 40% higher response rate. (LinkedIn Signal 31)

**Volume scaling rule:**
- 50,000 leads/month = test 8 angles
- 2,000 leads/month = test 4 angles max

> "Your first guess is rarely the one that wins."
> -- LinkedIn Signal 5

## Positioning Against the "Industry Quo"

Reframe your offer to contrast with what the industry actively hates.

| Industry | Status Quo Pain | Positioning | Source |
|----------|----------------|-------------|--------|
| Lawyers | TV ads and billboards | "without spending a cent on TV ads or billboards" | NLM Signal 29 |
| Bookkeeping | Slow CPAs | "Gen Z accounting firm" with 24/7 Slack/text access | NLM Signal 29 |
| Lead gen | Everyone offers cold email only | "mix of LinkedIn, cold email, and warm calling" | NLM Signal 53 |

This creates differentiation without changing the actual service. (NLM Signal 29)

## Radical Transparency (Zero Social Proof)

When you have no case studies, no portfolio, no testimonials:

1. **Admit it:** "Full transparency, I'm a brand new agency owner and am looking to gain experience." Pattern interrupt because nobody else admits this. (NLM Signal 16)
2. **Absorb all risk:** "I'll cover all costs and take on all the risk and you get the upside." (NLM Signal 16)
3. **Do free work:** "I'm confident I can outperform your current agency, I'm willing to do free work to prove myself." (NLM Signal 16)

> "I would go as far as saying that you're inherently selfish if you aren't willing to do free work to prove yourself."
> -- LinkedIn Signal 24 `[V]`

> "People who are far inferior to you are stealing business from you just because they built more goodwill with the prospect."
> -- LinkedIn Signal 24 `[V]`

## Agent Instructions

When constructing an offer:
1. Start with market diagnosis (Level 1/2/3 or four-tier model). This determines everything downstream.
2. Apply XYZ Framework for base structure.
3. Layer on Intangible + Concrete + Proof.
4. If commoditized, run the angle invention process (break down components, find unpitched angle).
5. Test 4 angles minimum before declaring a winner.
6. Never state pricing in email unless the price itself is the advantage.
7. For high-ticket ($100K+), recommend the Paid Scoping Sprint.
8. For zero-social-proof situations, deploy Radical Transparency.

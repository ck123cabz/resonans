## The $2,000 line decides the funnel

- **Type:** [OFFER] / [FUNNEL]
- **Provenance:** [D], stated as his rule
- **Date:** 2025-10-07
- **Source:** `video/the-only-funnel-training-you-ll-ever-need-1m-funnel.md`
- "there's kind of a line in the sand these rules are always broken but really anything under $2,000 is going to fall into a different funnel than anything above $2,000".

## Call or no call: when the sales call goes

- **Type:** [OFFER]
- **Provenance:** [X:Taki Moore] for the model; [D] for Ryan's conditions
- **Date:** 2026-04-16
- **Source:** `video/this-funnel-generates-1-6m-mo-without-sales-calls.md`
- Taki Moore sells a $2,000-then-$2,500-a-month offer with no call: long application, $1,500 refundable deposit, sold out 9 months running. His reason: "trust has moved upstream in 2026".
- Taki's principles as Ryan lists them: close doors on purpose, "scarcity has to be real", "content is the new sales call for offers like this".
- Ryan's limit: "if you have a very custom offer if it's done for you it doesn't work quite well". His own agency keeps the call because its services are custom.
- Waitlist vs application: name and email until you can sell out; application and deposit once demand exceeds supply.

## Price tests: crossing a round number moves volume

- **Type:** [OFFER] / [METRIC]
- **Provenance:** [D]
- **Date:** 2026-09-11; 2026-06-05
- **Source:** `video/the-easiest-way-to-double-your-sales-funnel-revenue.md`; `video/i-ran-200-sales-funnel-experiments-here-s-what-converts.md`
- Down: $149 → $99 (+112% revenue; another client tripled sales); $297 → $197 won; $197 → $147 did nothing. Up: $200 → $247 raised conversion and revenue (Joel Turner); $15 → $30 a month "literally doubled the revenue".
- Single pricing tests across clients: "111% increase in revenue ... 108% increase in conversion rate ... 43% increase in sales ... 23% increases in revenue".
- Judge by earnings per visitor: "the conversion rate is way higher but who cares".
- Phone price tests are not clean, because reps say a new price with less confidence. Workaround: a new rep sells the higher price first.
- Price as signal [X:Cialdini]: a turquoise-jewelry shop doubled a price by mistake and sold out.

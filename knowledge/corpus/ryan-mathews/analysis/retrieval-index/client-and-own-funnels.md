## Steve, A Life Engineered: the $1.3M VSL funnel

- **Type:** [CASE]
- **Provenance:** [D]; Steve's career figures [X:Steve] as Ryan relays them
- **Date:** 2026-03-05 (main); also 2025-10-07, 2025-12-02, 2026-02-03
- **Source:** `video/the-only-vsl-funnel-training-you-ll-ever-need-1-3m-vsl.md`; `video/the-only-funnel-training-you-ll-ever-need-1m-funnel.md`; `video/how-to-create-a-1m-video-sales-letter-vsl.md`; `video/alex-hormozi-s-advice-for-sales-funnels.md`
- Who: ex-Amazon principal engineer coaching software engineers to promotion. Program "Top Tier".
- Traffic: YouTube, LinkedIn, Substack, X, a podcast; Meta ads added at "very low spend in 2026" with the same VSL on a duplicated page.
- Page, top to bottom: eyebrow "former Amazon principal engineer" → headline with offer and risk reversal → Vidalytics VSL → "Apply now" button anchoring to the survey → big-tech logo bar ("we've helped clients get promoted at") → on-page survey (DQ: not technical, junior or intern, under 2 years at level, location; LinkedIn URL and phone) → testimonials. Qualified → "you're pre-qualified" booking page → thank-you page with a confirm video → call. Disqualified → free Skool community.
- Offer: "a high performance review or promotion in 12 months or work with you for free until you do". No price appears anywhere on the page or in the VSL.
- Results: "in the past 141 days it's generated $1.3 million in revenue and over 1,800 leads". Meta "9.5x return on ad spend so far", first-week cost per booked call $11. Logo bar A/B tested at +10%. Earlier VSL "averaging $6,500 a day in sales" and later "over $6,000 a day".
- The live VSL is "the fourth VSSL we've tested over the course of the last 4 months". Ryan wrote the first from an interview; Steve rewrote it into his own words.
- Ryan's fixes: a better hook ("just cut out that first six words"), a simpler headline, "see if I qualify" CTA, a second CTA under reviews, testimonials led by company, title and result. A benefit-worded button "slightly lowered conversion rate i think it was probably just too wordy".
- His framing: the funnel wins on message-, copy- and offer-market fit. The L6→L7 jump is worth "300k per year", about "$1,000 a day" of delay.

- **Type:** [CASE]

- Offer pivot: ops coaching sold "like selling people a gym membership. They know they need it, but they don't really want it." Switching to client acquisition worked "like a light switch".
- Headline: "Discover how we can guarantee you 12K in your MR in 12 weeks" (as captioned; 12K in new MRR); sub-headline "without ads trade shows or any new employees"; eyebrow "MSP owners only". Proof line "trusted by 100 MSP owners" under the button.

QFNB email 30 minutes after a qualified non-booker; his EA books replies.
- Traffic: Facebook ads, plus a Reddit test because Reddit is "very very very cheap" for this market. Results not yet reported.
- VSL modeled on a 5-minute Charlie Morgan agency VSL and a Cole Gordon transcript. The first AI draft "is just using too much of Cole's actual language".
- Mobile: some clients get 96% of traffic on mobile; check mobile first.

## The Frankenfunnel client: $7K to $240K a month, and the VSL that tanked

- **Type:** [CASE]
- **Provenance:** [D]; the first-visit discount idea [X:Justin Welsh] via the client
- **Date:** 2025-09-10
- **Source:** `video/repaired-transcript-i-made-this-business-owner-249-000-mo-learned-this.md`
- Low-ticket front end, high-ticket back end on calls. "When this client first came to me, they had what I would call a franken funnel." A year of tests took conversion from under 0.5% to 3.68%; front end held 2x ROAS while scaling. A 3-day first-visit flash sale doubled organic conversion and added about 27% on ads.
- Failed variant: they scrapped the low ticket and sent everyone to a VSL-to-call funnel. "the calls were converting at 30% rather than 60%. So we were getting half the clients for the same amount of calls." The client lost $30K–50K of low-ticket revenue: worst month of the year.
- Ads: the client's high-production skit ads beat the selfie style Ryan advised. Lesson: "So the lesson here is optimize before you scale."

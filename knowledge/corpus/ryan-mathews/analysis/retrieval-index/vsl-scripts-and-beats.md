  2. Problem plus a filter: "stuck at mid senior or above", so juniors drop out.

  4. Story and dream outcome: "my comp jumped by over 300K per year".

  6. Offer with a guarantee per avatar: promotion or a better review in 12 months "or work with you for free until you do".
  7. Proof: "hundreds of engineers across Amazon Google Meta Airbnb".
  8. Reviews and testimonials.
  9. Objections.
  10. CTA.
- His summary: "it's not that complicated of a VSSL". No price is spoken; the guarantee is the offer.
- This is the only skeleton in the corpus with real money behind it ($1.3M in 141 days), so it is the stronger template for a booked-call funnel.

## Hormozi's 5 Ps: Promise, Pain, Proof, Plan, Picture

- **Type:** [VSL]
- **Provenance:** [X:Hormozi] structure; [D] for Ryan's lawn-care wording
- **Date:** 2026-05-07
- **Source:** `video/the-psychology-of-a-persuasive-video-sales-letter-vsl.md`
- Used when prospects do not yet know your type of solution. Intro in four beats, Promise → Pain → Proof → Plan; then the body runs the Ps again, expanded: Proof → Promise → Pain → Plan → Picture.
- Promise = outcome + timeframe + "without" the thing they dread. His example: "how we turn patchy weedy embarrassing lawns into thick green head turning grass".
- Pain: "the knife's kind of in now we're going to twist it". Proof must be specific and visual: "the burden of proof is on us to prove that what we're saying is true".
- Plan: a spoken agenda in the intro; in the body it is "the bridge to get from where they are to where they want to go". Explaining why the old way fails builds the unique mechanism: "you're feeding a patient that's actually sick underneath".
- Picture gets one clause ("establish a little bit more of a picture ... tying in some social proof"). How to write it is not covered. [A]
- He says the intro is built the same way as his YouTube intros.

## Script process, five steps (the $1M VSL method)

- **Type:** [VSL] / [AI]
- **Provenance:** [D]; modeling step [X:Brunson]
- **Date:** 2025-12-02
- **Source:** `video/how-to-create-a-1m-video-sales-letter-vsl.md`
- 1. Six inputs: audience, main problem, solution, price or commitment, credibility, guarantee. Probe each pain layers deep ("unreliable growth" → layoffs, replaced by AI). Dictate with Wispr Flow.
- 2. Pick a proven VSL to model, structure only. Filters: same traffic source, same audience, same offer type (which "sometimes trumps similar audience"), proven. Proof is the Meta Ad Library: an ad running since June 21 counts; one launched 7 days ago does not.
- 3. One prompt with the model's structure, the offer details and a tone guide from 5–10 YouTube transcripts: "do not copy the wording learn from and model the structure". Prompt text in `claude-and-ai-workflows.md`.
- 4. Several drafts in a Google Doc, combined or used as inspiration.
- 5. "a meeting in a few days where you can come back and look at it with fresh eyes", line by line with the client, then test and repeat.
- Worked example: MSP Fuel, modeled on a 5-minute Charlie Morgan VSL. Steve's VSL was written the same way: "this VSSL is averaging $6,500 a day in sales and it was one of the easiest I've ever written".

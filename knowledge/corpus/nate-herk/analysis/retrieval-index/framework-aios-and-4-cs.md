# AIOS and the 4 C's

An AIOS is Nate Herk's name for an operating layer inside a harness (Claude Code, Codex, Grok Bots, Hermes) that joins your context, files, APIs, schedules and skills. The 4 C's (Context, Connections, Capabilities, Cadence) are how he says to build one. The term runs across 45 transcripts from 2025-09-24 to 2026-09-24. The 4 C's arrive with his course on 2026-05-01 and are the flagship of his third era.

## AIOS: what he means by it, and "AIS OS"
`[F] · [D] · uploaded 2026-05-01 · build-sell-claude-code-operating-systems-2-hour-course.md · q37 · stable`

He treats a second brain as the first half of an AIOS. "AIS OS" is his pun on AI Automation Society, the name of his free community and of the kit he hands out.

> a second brain is a very great start to building an AIOS so there's a lot of these things that you might have heard and this is just kind of my take on an AIOS which is why I'm calling it the AIS OS (build-sell-claude-code-operating-systems-2-hour-course.md)

The earliest caption use, on 2025-09-24, pushes it aside: "I'm not talking about AI operating systems or regular automations i'm talking about diagnosing problems" (stop-selling-ai-agents-sell-ai-solutions-instead.md). Eight months later it is his main product idea.

## 4 C's: built in order, 1 to 4
`[R] · [D] · uploaded 2026-05-01 · build-sell-claude-code-operating-systems-2-hour-course.md · q50 · stable`

Each C depends on the one before. His test for C1: open a fresh session, ask a question, and see if it answers like a teammate.

> you can't have cadence without connections you can't have capability without context and you have to go in this order 1 2 3 4 (build-sell-claude-code-operating-systems-2-hour-course.md)

## 4 C's: context and connections are the second brain
`[F] · [D] · uploaded 2026-09-07 · i-turned-gpt-6-astra-into-the-ultimate-ai-second-brain.md · q50 · stable`

He splits the four into two halves. C1 and C2 make the second brain; C3 and C4 make the AIOS proper.

> I think about these first two things as the second brain piece so I think of this as your second brain and then over here we have the actual AIOS (i-turned-gpt-6-astra-into-the-ultimate-ai-second-brain.md)

Context is who you are, the business, priorities and the wiki. Connections are the MCPs, APIs and CLIs into tools like Gmail, Slack and ClickUp. The file names Gemini lists for context (`about_business.md`, `about_me.md`, `priorities.md`) are [M]; pass 2 found no caption for the `MI.md` routing line.

## 4 C's: capabilities means your SOPs
`[F] · [D] · uploaded 2026-05-01 · build-sell-claude-code-operating-systems-2-hour-course.md · q02, q46 · stable`

Capabilities are skills built from the SOPs a business already has, so the OS does a task as well as the employee who does it by hand.

> the capabilities is that it actually knows how to do things you know you have a bunch of SOPs probably already in your business can you now give those SOPs to your operating system (build-sell-claude-code-operating-systems-2-hour-course.md)

How he writes those skills is in `framework-6-step-skill-method-and-bike-method.md`.

## 4 C's: cadence means it runs with the laptop off
`[F] · [D] · uploaded 2026-05-01 · build-sell-claude-code-operating-systems-2-hour-course.md · q46 · stable`

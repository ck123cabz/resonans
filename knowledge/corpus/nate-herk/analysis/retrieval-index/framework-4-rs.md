# 4 Rs (Result, Roadmap, Risk Reversal, Review)

The 4 Rs is Nate Herk's offer-building framework for a first client. He teaches it in one video, "How to Sign Your First AI Automation Client (Without Starting an Agency)", uploaded 2025-11-17. Its aim is an offer so good the prospect "would feel stupid saying no", a nod to the cover line of Alex Hormozi's "$100M Offers".

## The four letters and the aim
`[F] · [D] · uploaded 2025-11-17 · how-to-sign-your-first-ai-automation-client-without-starting-an-agency.md · q42, q50 · stable`

> you want your offer to be so good that people would feel stupid saying no the easiest way to think about this is with a simple framework i call the four rs result road map risk reversal and review (how-to-sign-your-first-ai-automation-client-without-starting-an-agency.md)

He names all four in one breath, then walks each in order. The same video also carries his 60/30/10 golden ratio (see `framework-golden-ratio-60-30-10.md`).

## R1 Result: one workflow, one number
`[S] · [D] · uploaded 2025-11-17 · how-to-sign-your-first-ai-automation-client-without-starting-an-agency.md · q42 · stable`

The first R is the outcome. He says to pick one workflow you can deliver with confidence and state it as time or cost saved. His example: 90% of onboarding automated, 5 hours cut to 30 minutes.

> i can automate 90% of your onboarding process if it currently takes you 5 hours i can bring that down to 30 minutes always lead with a clear time or cost-saving result (how-to-sign-your-first-ai-automation-client-without-starting-an-agency.md)

## R2 Roadmap: explain point A to point B
`[S] · [D] · uploaded 2025-11-17 · how-to-sign-your-first-ai-automation-client-without-starting-an-agency.md · q42 · stable`

The second R is how you will deliver. He says the tool does not matter: n8n, Make, Zapier or OpenAI's Agent Builder all qualify. What the prospect needs is a clear path from where they are to the result.

> the second r is roadmap this is how you'll deliver the outcome the tool doesn't matter (how-to-sign-your-first-ai-automation-client-without-starting-an-agency.md)

> what matters is that you can explain how you'll get them from point a to b clarity builds trust (how-to-sign-your-first-ai-automation-client-without-starting-an-agency.md)

## R3 Risk reversal: free build or money back
`[J] · [D] · uploaded 2025-11-17 · how-to-sign-your-first-ai-automation-client-without-starting-an-agency.md · q42, q50 · stable`

The third R lowers the buyer's risk while you have no proof. His options: a free build in exchange for a testimonial, a money-back guarantee, or a pay-only-if-satisfied model.

> the third r is for risk reversal especially when you're just starting out make your offer low risk you can start with maybe a free build in exchange for testimonial or offer a money back (how-to-sign-your-first-ai-automation-client-without-starting-an-agency.md)

## R4 Review: objections are hidden costs
`[J] · [D] · uploaded 2025-11-17 · how-to-sign-your-first-ai-automation-client-without-starting-an-agency.md · q42 · stable`

The fourth R is a feedback loop on the offer. Write down every objection and question you get while pitching. He calls them hidden costs: each one names something the offer is missing, and it goes back into the next pitch. The "hidden costs" phrase recurs in the pricing retrospective (2026-08-01) and the 10-hour course (2026-03-12).

> these objections are clues showing you what's missing from your current offer and we call these hidden costs (how-to-sign-your-first-ai-automation-client-without-starting-an-agency.md)

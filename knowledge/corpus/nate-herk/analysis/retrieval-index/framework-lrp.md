# LRP (Listen, Repeat, Poke)

LRP is Nate Herk's name for the core of a discovery call. He names it in 6 transcripts, from the live sales call on 2025-09-07 to the Codex course on 2026-09-21. The full call, with opener, pokes and objection replies, sits in `script-discovery-call.md`.

## LRP defined in his words
`[F] · [D] · uploaded 2025-09-07; 2025-09-24; 2026-03-12 · how-i-sold-a-6000-ai-workflow-to-a-business-live-sales-call.md; stop-selling-ai-agents-sell-ai-solutions-instead.md; build-sell-with-claude-code-10-hour-course.md · q37, q42, q50 · stable`

Three moves, in order. Listen while the prospect describes their week or a process. Repeat the pattern back so they confirm you heard it. Poke with questions that put a number on it. He first named it on the live sales call of 2025-09-07.

> i use a framework that i call the lrp which stands for listen repeat poke (how-i-sold-a-6000-ai-workflow-to-a-business-live-sales-call.md)

> i would then use the lrp framework the listen repeat poke this basically means that i listen while they describe their week or their processes i repeat back the pattern to confirm alignment and then i poke to quantify it (build-sell-with-claude-code-10-hour-course.md)

The same wording opens the discovery section of "Stop Selling AI Agents, Sell AI Solutions Instead" (2025-09-24), and the 10-hour course (2026-03-12) reuses it word for word. Count those two as one origin.

## The 70/30 talk ratio
`[R] · [D] · uploaded 2025-09-07 · how-i-sold-a-6000-ai-workflow-to-a-business-live-sales-call.md · q22, q44 · stable`

On the live call he states the ratio the Listen step depends on: the prospect talks 70% of the time. He pairs it with keeping the talk on business value.

> listen for 70% of the time speak for 30% of the time focus conversation on business value not technical details (how-i-sold-a-6000-ai-workflow-to-a-business-live-sales-call.md)

## Silence after the question
`[J] · [D] · uploaded 2026-08-25; 2026-09-21 · the-3-step-system-behind-my-20k-month-ai-agency-retainers.md; build-sell-with-codex-5-hour-course.md · q44 · stable`

In the retainer video of 2026-08-25 he says he asked a prospect why she booked the call, then stopped talking. He held the pause twice because he did not trust the first answer.

> there's just a lot of power in silence so when i asked her the question of like why did you book this call like what is truly the pain that you're feeling here i stopped talking (build-sell-with-codex-5-hour-course.md)

## Poke: sizing questions measure the ceiling
`[S] · [D] · uploaded 2026-08-01 · 18-months-of-pricing-ai-automations-in-21-mins.md · q20, q44 · stable`

In the pricing retrospective (2026-08-01) the Poke step is a set of sizing questions. How long does the task take, how many people touch it, what happens when it goes wrong. He says these size the value ceiling, which then feeds the 10% to 20% price anchor (see `framework-price.md`).

> how many people touch it and what happens when it goes wrong these are all sizing questions they measure the ceiling not the build (18-months-of-pricing-ai-automations-in-21-mins.md)

When a prospect will not share salaries, he pokes with proxies instead:

> how many locations do you have or how many of these come in on your busiest days (18-months-of-pricing-ai-automations-in-21-mins.md)

## Poke by category: money, focus, errors
`[S] · [D] · uploaded 2025-09-24 · stop-selling-ai-agents-sell-ai-solutions-instead.md · q44 · stable`

The 2025-09-24 video groups pokes by what the business loses. Money: who is paid to copy and paste. Focus: what interrupts the morning. Errors: where mistakes cause rework, refunds or churn.

> where are you paying people to copy and paste or chase info you could talk about focus what interrupts you the most between 9 and noon you can talk about errors where do mistakes cause rework refunds or churn (stop-selling-ai-agents-sell-ai-solutions-instead.md)

## After LRP: take notes, then pivot
`[F] · [D] · uploaded 2026-08-01 · 18-months-of-pricing-ai-automations-in-21-mins.md · q44 · stable`

LRP fills the middle of the call. Once it has done its job he takes notes and pivots to the best case, then runs the three buckets: why this, why now, why me. Those buckets and the "who watches it at 2 in the morning" reply are in `script-discovery-call.md`.

> your job is to just listen repeat and poke and just run that lrp framework to get as much information as you can take a ton of notes and then you pivot (18-months-of-pricing-ai-automations-in-21-mins.md)

## [D:guest:Sav] Sending infrastructure: 150 a day, then 450, then 40 to 50 inboxes
`[R] · [D:guest:Sav] · uploaded 2026-02-14 · how-a-college-student-made-500k-with-cold-email-exact-framework.md · q26 · version-bound`

He started on 1 domain with 5 inboxes at 30 sends each: 150 a day once warmed. He says the usual advice is 2 or 3 inboxes per domain and he would not advise 5. Three domains triples it to 450 a day, about 9,000 a month. Today he runs 40 to 50 inboxes. The "450 a day from LinkedIn lists, zero clients" failure in q26 is student Christian's story from a different video, not Sav's.

> I literally bought one domain and I wouldn't advise this but I I got like five email inboxes under each (how-a-college-student-made-500k-with-cold-email-exact-framework.md)

> what is 30 times 5 um 150 right and basically I would send out 150 emails a day from these accounts once they were like fully warmed up (how-a-college-student-made-500k-with-cold-email-exact-framework.md)

> I probably have like I want to say like 40 to 50 inboxes running at any given time (how-a-college-student-made-500k-with-cold-email-exact-framework.md)

He skips SPF, DKIM and DMARC setup on camera as "a massive rabbit hole". Warm-up duration is never given.

## [D:guest:Sav] Reply-rate tiers: under 2%, 2 to 5%, 5 to 10%
`[R] · [D:guest:Sav] · uploaded 2026-02-14 · how-a-college-student-made-500k-with-cold-email-exact-framework.md · q26 · stable`

Under 2%: check the lead list and deliverability. 2% to 5%: the copy and offer. 5% to 10%: the golden zone. Above 10% is Gemini's "elite" label.

> below 2% look back at your lead list look back at your infrastructure above 2% below 5% look at your copy and your offer between 5 and 10% you're probably good (how-a-college-student-made-500k-with-cold-email-exact-framework.md)

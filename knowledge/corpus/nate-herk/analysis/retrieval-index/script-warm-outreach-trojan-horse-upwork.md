# Warm outreach, Trojan Horse and Upwork scripts

The lines Nate gives beginners for finding a first client without an audience. Channel order in his teaching: warm network first, then partners (Trojan Horse), then Upwork, then cold outreach. Sources run from 2025-11-17 to 2026-08-11. Cold email is in script-cold-email.md.

## 7-day plan, day 1: write down 20 people
`[S] · [D] · uploaded 2026-02-04 · how-to-sign-your-first-ai-automation-client-in-7-days-with-proof.md · q23 · stable`

Day 1 is a list of 20 people where trust already exists: friends and family with businesses, past co-workers and managers, community members, second-degree connections. Note each one's industry and how well you know them.

> your goal for day one would be to write down 20 people that you can reach out to friends or family who run businesses past co-workers managers clients people who you know from communities (how-to-sign-your-first-ai-automation-client-in-7-days-with-proof.md)

## Warm opener: "I'm not trying to sell you anything"
`[S] · [D] · uploaded 2026-02-04; 2026-03-12 · how-to-sign-your-first-ai-automation-client-in-7-days-with-proof.md; build-sell-with-claude-code-10-hour-course.md · q22, q23 · stable`

Days 2 and 3 are conversations. He says to sound like a curious entrepreneur. The q22 follow-up for contacts with no business ("Is there anyone in your network who might benefit from this?") was not found in the windows read and stays [M].

> Hey I'm trying to start a business where I help other businesses automate repetitive work with AI i'm not trying to sell you anything could I just ask you a few questions about where things feel manual or annoying in your day-to-day? (how-to-sign-your-first-ai-automation-client-in-7-days-with-proof.md)

## Free pilot ask, days 4 to 6
`[S] · [D] · uploaded 2026-02-04 · how-to-sign-your-first-ai-automation-client-in-7-days-with-proof.md · q23 · stable`

Offer the contact with the clearest pain a small free build in exchange for honest feedback. Build the simplest thing that works. On day 7, if it went well, ask for an intro; if not, take the feedback and do not ask for a testimonial.

> Hey I'd love to build you a small automation that tackles X painoint as a free pilot my goal here is just to prove that these workflows actually can save you time and in return I'd just ask for some honest feedback (how-to-sign-your-first-ai-automation-client-in-7-days-with-proof.md)

The student in that video, Christian (21, Arizona), closed $1,500 five days after joining and later raised it to $2,000. That is one student. q45's "members frequently close pilots in 5 to 7 days" is Gemini's generalization.

## Trojan Horse partner pitch, 20% of project revenue
`[S] · [D] · uploaded 2026-03-12 · build-sell-with-claude-code-10-hour-course.md · q22, q23, q42 · stable`

Borrow a partner's trust: offer free AI discovery calls to a marketing agency's, consultant's or law firm's clients, and pay the partner 20% of any project that follows. In the 10-hour course he says this is the method he would go all in on starting from zero. q42's Shopify agency example with 10 clients and $5,000 builds has no caption; it is Gemini's illustration.

> hey I help businesses implement AI automation i'd love to offer free AI discovery calls to your clients no cost to you no cost to them you get to look like a hero because you brought in an AI expert (build-sell-with-claude-code-10-hour-course.md)

> and if any of them end up wanting to work with me I'll give you 20% of that project revenue (build-sell-with-claude-code-10-hour-course.md)

## Ask to send a 2-minute Loom, not for 30 minutes
`[S] · [D] · uploaded 2026-02-16 · how-to-sign-ai-workflow-clients-with-0-followers.md · q22, q23 · stable`

A stranger's calendar is a big ask. Ask to send a short Loom walking through the fix for their business. The 90-second version is in Sav's cold email (script-cold-email.md).

> asking a stranger for 30 minutes on their calendar is a big ask ... ask them if you can send them a 2-minute Loom video explaining the offer (how-to-sign-ai-workflow-clients-with-0-followers.md)

## Warm-network outreach prompt, Gemini's version
`[S] · [M] · uploaded n/a · no transcript · q39 · stable`

[M] Gemini's version only; no caption carries this text.

```text
Act as a B2B outreach copywriter. Write me a casual, friendly first-touch message for [Contact Name].
I am a one-person AI consultant and I just built [Name/type of automation].
My goal is NOT to pitch this prospect. It is to get on a 20-minute call to learn about their
business, ask about their daily operational bottlenecks, and show them what I built. Keep it under
100 words, no corporate speak, no buzzwords like 'synergy' or 'leverage'. Write it like a real
person who actually built something.
```

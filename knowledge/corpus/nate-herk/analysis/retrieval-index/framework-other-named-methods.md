# Other named methods

Nate Herk's smaller named methods, one heading each. All are his own unless marked. The last section lists names Gemini invented, which must not be cited as his.

## 3 M's of AI: mindset, method, machine
`[F] · [D] · uploaded 2026-05-01 · build-sell-claude-code-operating-systems-2-hour-course.md · q37, q46 · stable`

A high-level frame he reads from a doc he drops in his free community. 4 files, 2026-05-01 to 2026-09-23. Its line on mindset: treat AI "as a mentor not a vending machine".

> it's called the three m's of ai we've got the mindset the method and the machine (build-sell-claude-code-operating-systems-2-hour-course.md)

## 3 Ps: pain, person, promise
`[F] · [D] · uploaded 2026-09-18 · anthropic-s-ceo-how-to-build-a-1-person-business-with-claude.md · q37, q42 · stable`

Before launching a service or SaaS, settle one pain, one person, one promise. He also calls it the "AI monetization readiness assessment". On camera he corrects his own order to pain, person, promise. 9 files, 2026-02-04 to 2026-09-21.

> what i call the ai monetization readiness assessment which is the three ps pain promise person actually no i like to go pain person promise (anthropic-s-ceo-how-to-build-a-1-person-business-with-claude.md)

## 6 Ps of a SaaS sprint
`[F] · [D] · uploaded 2026-08-10 · build-sell-ai-saas-products-2-hour-course.md · q50 · stable`

The build order in his Client Pack course: pain, promise, product, plumbing, packaging, proof. Plumbing means payments, auth and the database. Gemini's list (Person, Pricing, Promotion) is wrong on three of six.

> we have the pain we need to start building out the promise the product the plumbing and the packaging (build-sell-ai-saas-products-2-hour-course.md)

## Four blanks: bucket, KPI, baseline, 60-day target
`[F] · [D] · uploaded 2026-08-11 · how-to-build-a-one-person-ai-business-using-claude-code.md · q42 · stable`

Every automation has to fill four blanks or it is not a project. The bucket is one of three: more customers, each customer worth more, lower costs.

> this automation is in the x bucket the specific kpi is xmetric the baseline today is x number and after 60 days we expect x target four blanks (how-to-build-a-one-person-ai-business-using-claude-code.md)

## Doctor, not pharmacist
`[F] · [D] · uploaded 2026-03-08 to 2026-03-12 · build-sell-with-claude-code-10-hour-course.md · q31, q44 · stable`

A pharmacist fills someone else's order ("build me an n8n bot"). A doctor diagnoses where the business leaks time or money first. In 8+ transcripts.

> act as the doctor not the pharmacist i've used this analogy a lot on my channel a pharmacist just fills a prescription that someone else wrote (build-sell-with-claude-code-10-hour-course.md)

## Default shift: how could AI do 30% of it
`[F] · [D] · uploaded 2026-05-01 · build-sell-claude-code-operating-systems-2-hour-course.md · q32 · stable`

Before any task, ask how AI could do it, or at least 30% of it. Gemini's "30% to 50%" is wrong; he says 30%.

> the first one is the default shift basically the idea of you know before you do any task just ask yourself how could ai do this (build-sell-claude-code-operating-systems-2-hour-course.md)

## Vending machine versus slot machine
`[F] · [D] · uploaded 2026-02-27 to 2026-08-14 · claude-code-for-non-coders-6-hour-course.md · q31 · stable`

A workflow is a vending machine: same input, same output. An agent is a slot machine. 7 files. He uses "vending machine" two ways: as praise for deterministic architecture, and as the wrong way to prompt ("a mentor not a vending machine"). Pass 2 (X-10) keeps both.

> a vending machine is deterministic you put in a quarter you hit e4 you get a coke same input same output (claude-code-for-non-coders-6-hour-course.md)

## The 10-minute rule for n8n
`[J] · [D] · uploaded 2026-02-01 · is-n8n-dead.md · q41 · version-bound`

If a use case builds in n8n in 10 minutes or less, build it there. Past that, move to Claude Code.

> my 10-minute rule is basically if i have a use case and i could build it in 10 minutes or less in nit i'm just going to do it there (is-n8n-dead.md)

## Reactive prompting
`[J] · [D] · uploaded 2025-02-26 to 2025-07-25 · your-ai-agent-prompts-are-wrong-here-s-the-fix.md · q32 · stable`

Start with a short prompt, run it, and add a rule only when a real error shows up. 5 files. He says he stopped using his own system-prompt generator because it was proactive.

> the biggest lesson that i've learned while prompting ai agents which is prompting needs to be done reactively i see way too many people doing this proactively (your-ai-agent-prompts-are-wrong-here-s-the-fix.md)

## Walk it down the model list
`[F] · [D] · uploaded 2026-09-19; 2026-09-21 · how-to-build-codex-skills-better-than-99-of-people.md; build-sell-with-codex-5-hour-course.md · q15, q34 · version-bound`

Set the quality bar on a top model, then step down the model list and effort levels until quality drops. Keep the cheapest one that holds. His example pair is Astra over Sol.

> when i say walk it down i mean walking it down the model list (build-sell-with-codex-5-hour-course.md)

## Fix the access, not the prompt
`[J] · [D] · uploaded 2026-08-04 · 5000-hours-of-building-ai-in-just-17-minutes.md · q31, q33 · stable`

A prompt rule is a suggestion; a tool rule is a restriction. If the agent has a send-email tool, "only write drafts" will not stop it. Gemini's "prompts are suggestions, tools are walls" is not his wording.

> a rule that lives in the prompt is just a suggestion but a rule that lives in the tools is an actual restriction (5000-hours-of-building-ai-in-just-17-minutes.md)

> if the answer scares you then fix the access not the prompt (5000-hours-of-building-ai-in-just-17-minutes.md)

## One real task, and the 20% dip
`[J] · [D] · uploaded 2026-07-03; 2026-05-01 · how-claude-is-creating-a-new-generation-of-millionaires.md; build-sell-claude-code-operating-systems-2-hour-course.md · q32 · stable`

Two learning rules without formal names. Pick one real task, not ten. And expect productivity to fall about 20% after any change before it rises.

> step two pick one real task not 10 just pick one (how-claude-is-creating-a-new-generation-of-millionaires.md)

> whenever you make a change you typically are going to expect a 20% decrease in productivity (build-sell-claude-code-operating-systems-2-hour-course.md)

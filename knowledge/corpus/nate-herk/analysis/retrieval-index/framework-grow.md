# GROW framework

GROW is Nate Herk's four-step plan for stage 3 of his start-over video (2025-10-03): scaling solo consulting into an agency he calls an AI partner. The letters are Get developers, Retain authority, Onboard sales, Win with brand. It appears in that one file. Gemini's q37 got three of the four letters wrong; the corrected letters below come from the captions.

## GROW: the corrected letters
`[F] · [D] · uploaded 2025-10-03 · how-i-d-make-money-with-ai-in-2026-if-i-had-to-start-over.md · q37, q50 · stable`

> This one is the grow framework The G stands for get developers (how-i-d-make-money-with-ai-in-2026-if-i-had-to-start-over.md)

G is Get developers, R is Retain authority, O is Onboard sales, W is Win with brand. Gemini's q37 version reads "Re-invest in sales, Offer high-ticket blueprints, Win managed partnerships". The blueprint and revenue-share ideas in that version belong to Devin Karns (section at the end). Never use q37's letters.

## GROW: stage 3, from consultant to AI partner
`[F] · [D] · uploaded 2025-10-03 · how-i-d-make-money-with-ai-in-2026-if-i-had-to-start-over.md · q24 · stable`

He hit a ceiling as a solo consultant: discovery calls plus delivery maxed out his hours. The answer was a team. At True Horizon they called themselves an AI partner because clients want long-term partnerships, not one-off projects. He scores the stage speed to money 5, ease for beginners 2, income potential 8, and says an agency on consulting foundations "can easily hit over $1 million in annual revenue".

> So stage three here is to scale into an AI partner business Unlike freelancing or solo consulting the partner model is about building a team around you (how-i-d-make-money-with-ai-in-2026-if-i-had-to-start-over.md)

He also tells beginners this is not a first step: "Don't run before you walk."

## GROW: the market he points at
`[J] · [D] · uploaded 2025-10-03 · how-i-d-make-money-with-ai-in-2026-if-i-had-to-start-over.md · q24 · stable`

He contrasts the big firms: McKinsey, BCG and Accenture charge "hundreds of thousands of dollars just for an AI consultation or audit". His pitch is to diagnose and build for mid-size companies at a fraction of that. He never says "avoid SMBs"; that line is Devin's.

> If you can walk into a mid-size company diagnose inefficiencies design the roadmap and then actually build the automations as well you can charge a fraction of what the big firms do (how-i-d-make-money-with-ai-in-2026-if-i-had-to-start-over.md)

# Proposal and tiers

How Nate writes a proposal and structures price after the discovery call. Most of it is in his pricing retrospective of 2026-08-01; the tier anchor and 50/50 terms come from 2025 videos. No full proposal document or contract template exists in the corpus; this is the set of rules and lines he speaks.

## Top of the proposal: three paragraphs about the client
`[S] · [D] · uploaded 2026-08-01 · 18-months-of-pricing-ai-automations-in-21-mins.md · q20, q45 · stable`

Three short paragraphs before any option. Where the business is now, in their words and numbers. Where they said they want to be. Why this gets them there. Nothing about him or his price.

> the top of that proposal is three short paragraphs and none of them are about you or your pricing they're about where the business is right now in their words with their numbers in it (18-months-of-pricing-ai-automations-in-21-mins.md)

## Three tiers at 10%, 25%, 50% of first-year value
`[R] · [D] · uploaded 2026-08-01 · 18-months-of-pricing-ai-automations-in-21-mins.md · q20, q45, q51 · stable`

Worked on a $100,000 first-year value: $10,000, $25,000, $50,000. He calls them rough numbers. The feature lists per tier in q20 are Gemini's.

> option one is 10% so $10,000 option two is 25% so 25,000 and option three is 50% 50 grand and I'm just kind of throwing out rough numbers here (18-months-of-pricing-ai-automations-in-21-mins.md)

## The middle tier is designed to win

Gemini's line that tiers turn "should I hire this person" into "which option" has no caption. It stays [M].

## Lead with the highest anchor: $25,000 scale, $12,000 growth
`[R] · [D] · uploaded 2025-08-31 · how-i-sold-these-4-ai-agents-for-23-000-as-a-beginner.md · q22 · stable`

The 2025-08-31 video names starter, growth and scale plans and quotes scale at $25,000 and growth at $12,000. No starter price is spoken. q22's "$5,000 starter" is Gemini's, and these dollar tiers carry no percentages.

> our scale package is 25,000 and it includes these functionalities but most clients actually go with the growth package at 12K that has these functionalities Suddenly that 12K feels like the smart middle option (how-i-sold-these-4-ai-agents-for-23-000-as-a-beginner.md)

## Assumption line for job-marketplace bids
`[S] · [D] · uploaded 2026-08-01 · 18-months-of-pricing-ai-automations-in-21-mins.md; build-sell-with-codex-5-hour-course.md · q45 · stable`

For a posted job, size it from the volume and headcount in the post, state the assumption, and offer a 15-minute call to adjust. He says "job marketplace" and does not name Upwork here. "Assumption Frame" is Gemini's label for it.

> I've priced this assuming that there's about 200 of these a month and you know if that's off let's hop on a 15-minute call and I can adjust the price and we can rework the scope a little bit (18-months-of-pricing-ai-automations-in-21-mins.md)

## Payment terms: three payments, 30 days apart, in 2026
`[R] · [D] · uploaded 2026-08-01 · 18-months-of-pricing-ai-automations-in-21-mins.md · q24 · stable`

By 2026-08-01 he set 30-day milestones and billed at each: one to start, one after the first milestone, one after the last. His example is a $9,000 project. He names no percentages; q24's "33/33/34" is Gemini's. Each milestone has to be objective enough that it "cannot be argued with".

> each milestone is 30 days apart and that's where you're going to get paid is essentially every 30 days then what you do is you could split this into three payments if we have two major milestones (18-months-of-pricing-ai-automations-in-21-mins.md)

## Scope creep: the version 2 backlog
`[S] · [D] · uploaded 2026-08-01 · 18-months-of-pricing-ai-automations-in-21-mins.md · q22 · stable`

A new ask mid-build is agreed with, then parked, so the agreed milestones ship first.

> let's go ahead and throw this on the backlog for our version two of the project and as you get more ideas you can just add more stuff to the backlog (18-months-of-pricing-ai-automations-in-21-mins.md)

## Budget objection: cut scope, not price
`[S] · [D] · uploaded 2026-08-01; 2025-11-30 · 18-months-of-pricing-ai-automations-in-21-mins.md; how-to-price-ai-workflows-without-losing-clients.md · q20, q22, q47 · stable`

> it sounds like 20 grand isn't in the budget right now why don't we just like reduce the scope a little bit and start with a smaller project (18-months-of-pricing-ai-automations-in-21-mins.md)

> if they push back adjust the scope not the price reduce complexity remove a feature break it into phases but don't discount the value (how-to-price-ai-workflows-without-losing-clients.md)

The script names no MVP price. q20's "$7,000 MVP" is Gemini's. The only $7,000 in his words is the anti-pattern on 2026-08-25, where he warns against "15k but for you we'll do it for 7k", while allowing a cheap first deal for reps.

> you don't want to get into this negotiation of devaluing your work and saying "ah you know this would be 15k but for you we'll do it for 7k." (the-3-step-system-behind-my-20k-month-ai-agency-retainers.md)

## Pass-through costs: client's account, client's card
`[R] · [D] · uploaded 2026-08-01 · 18-months-of-pricing-ai-automations-in-21-mins.md · q20, q45 · stable`

Tokens and hosting go on the client's own accounts. His fee covers design, consulting, building and testing. q20's "add $100 to $3,000 for test tokens" has no caption; during testing he says "we paid for everything".

> I have always said client's account client's card every single time your fee is for design consulting building testing now the tokens are a utility bill (18-months-of-pricing-ai-automations-in-21-mins.md)

## Who owns the IP: the client
`[R] · [D] · uploaded 2025-11-30 · how-to-price-ai-workflows-without-losing-clients.md · q47 · stable`

He answers yes, everything built is theirs, and says the real value sits in their prompts, data and workflows.

> clients want to know if they will own what you build now the simple answer is yes it's just the best way to handle it all IP that we build for them is theirs (how-to-price-ai-workflows-without-losing-clients.md)

> the real IP in the system is the prompts your data your workflows and how everything is combined (how-to-price-ai-workflows-without-losing-clients.md)

# AI Service Ladder

Nate Herk's ladder for selling AI services has four rungs, numbered 0 to 3: hours, audit, project, retainer. He teaches it in three videos: 2026-05-22, 2026-08-11 and 2026-08-31. The prices drift a little between the first two, and both versions are kept below.

## Service Ladder: the four rungs in one line
`[F] · [D] · uploaded 2026-08-11 · how-to-build-a-one-person-ai-business-using-claude-code.md · q21, q37 · stable`

> the rungs are this education or consulting then audit then project then retainer (how-to-build-a-one-person-ai-business-using-claude-code.md)

Gemini's q31 gives three rungs and q37 gives a retainer top of $20,000+. Both are corrected in pass 2: four rungs, and rung 3 tops out at $10,000.

## Service Ladder rung 0: selling hours, $100 to $500
`[R] · [D] · uploaded 2026-05-22; 2026-08-11 · the-ai-offer-you-can-sell-tomorrow-morning.md; how-to-build-a-one-person-ai-business-using-claude-code.md · q21 · version-bound`

Rung 0 is one hour or one session: teach the owner or team to use AI on real work, or set them up in Claude Code or Cowork. On 2026-05-22 the price is per session. On 2026-08-11 it is per hour, with $200 as his default.

> rung zero at the bottom is selling hours so consulting work maybe this could be $100 to $500 a session (the-ai-offer-you-can-sell-tomorrow-morning.md)

> for education or consulting hours you're probably going to charge anywhere from $100 to $500 per hour (how-to-build-a-one-person-ai-business-using-claude-code.md)

> $200 is a great default (how-to-build-a-one-person-ai-business-using-claude-code.md)

Rung 0 is optional for an existing client: fold education into the current project. Gemini's team-workshop price of $1,000 to $5,000 has no caption; treat it as unverified.

## Service Ladder rung 0 as a secret audit
`[J] · [D] · uploaded 2026-08-11 · how-to-build-a-one-person-ai-business-using-claude-code.md · q21 · stable`

While he sets up a client's AIOS, he learns where the business breaks. That makes the paid audit an easy next ask.

> the other thing that I love about rung zero is that it's basically a secret audit which is rung one (how-to-build-a-one-person-ai-business-using-claude-code.md)

## Service Ladder rung 1: the paid audit
`[R] · [D] · uploaded 2026-05-22; 2026-08-11 · the-ai-offer-you-can-sell-tomorrow-morning.md; how-to-build-a-one-person-ai-business-using-claude-code.md · q21 · version-bound`

Rung 1 is paid scoping: map workflows, find what can be automated, write the proposal for the first build. The top of the range moved from $2,500 to $3,000 in 81 days.

> rung one would be like an audit maybe $500 to 2500 bucks for paid scoping where you go in for like an hour or two (the-ai-offer-you-can-sell-tomorrow-morning.md)

> a flat fee for 500 bucks to maybe even 3,000 bucks depending on how deep you're going (how-to-build-a-one-person-ai-business-using-claude-code.md)

Do not add an "enterprise audit" of $5,000 to $35,000 to this rung. The $15,000 to $35,000 blueprint is Devin Karns's price at Custom AI Studio (`framework-borrowed-and-guest.md`), and no caption gives a $5,000 floor. Nate's own $5,000 audit belongs to the consulting stage of SCAN (`framework-scan.md`), a different video.

## Service Ladder rung 2: the first project, $2,500 to $10,000
`[R] · [D] · uploaded 2026-05-22 · the-ai-offer-you-can-sell-tomorrow-morning.md · q21 · version-bound`

One focused job, one workflow shipped end to end, numbers that prove ROI. Pricing inside the rung follows the 10x rule and the 10% to 20% anchor (`framework-price.md`).

> rung two is your first project that is you know obviously maybe 2500 to 10 grand for one focused scope of work where you ship a single workflow end to end (the-ai-offer-you-can-sell-tomorrow-morning.md)

His own early builds ($1,200 to $12,000) are listed in `build-early-agent-builds-ledger.md`.

## Service Ladder rung 3: retainers, $3,000 to $10,000 a month
`[R] · [D] · uploaded 2026-05-22; 2026-08-11 · the-ai-offer-you-can-sell-tomorrow-morning.md; how-to-build-a-one-person-ai-business-using-claude-code.md · q21 · version-bound`

> rung three which is where you want to be is retainers these can be maybe $3 to $10,000 a month ongoing (the-ai-offer-you-can-sell-tomorrow-morning.md)

> for a retainer you're maybe in the 3K to 10K a month range per month for a solo operator (how-to-build-a-one-person-ai-business-using-claude-code.md)

The $20,000 figure people attach to this rung is True Horizon's own minimum retainer, a fact about his old agency. Light maintenance of $200 to $1,500 per system and his flat $400 plan are a different thing from a rung 3 retainer (`script-10x-roi-pricing.md`).

## Service Ladder premise: earn each rung
`[J] · [D] · uploaded 2026-08-11; 2026-02-04 · how-to-build-a-one-person-ai-business-using-claude-code.md; how-to-sign-your-first-ai-automation-client-in-7-days-with-proof.md · q21, q23 · stable`

Beginners freeze because they pitch rung 3 with no proof.

> you have to earn each rung by standing on the one below it a lot of beginners are freezing because they're trying to skip straight from nothing to rung three (how-to-build-a-one-person-ai-business-using-claude-code.md)

> trust comes before retainers asking someone for a retainer right away would be like asking someone for a referral right away (how-to-sign-your-first-ai-automation-client-in-7-days-with-proof.md)

## Service Ladder: hourly is the on-ramp, not the business
`[J] · [D] · uploaded 2026-08-01; 2026-08-11 · 18-months-of-pricing-ai-automations-in-21-mins.md; how-to-build-a-one-person-ai-business-using-claude-code.md · q20 · stable`

Rung 0 sells hours while his pricing video says quit hourly. He squares it himself: hourly is fine for the first two or three projects. The case against hourly is Jonathan Stark's, relayed at AIS Live.

> I'm not saying hourly is never okay i think for your first two or three projects when you've got you know nothing to point at ... billing hourly is a safer ask (18-months-of-pricing-ai-automations-in-21-mins.md)

> you obviously don't want to be in the game forever of trading time for money but like I said it's the on-ramp (how-to-build-a-one-person-ai-business-using-claude-code.md)

## Service Ladder, Grok Bots version: free work first, audits $1,000 to 10,000
`[F] · [D] · uploaded 2026-08-31 · build-sell-grok-bots-2-hour-course.md · q17, q21 · version-bound`

His third telling adds a free rung under hourly work. Audits here run from $1,000 "to upwards of 10,000". A Grok Bot team setup fits the hourly or project rung, at about $5,000. Gemini's "$15k+ retainers" after that setup has no caption.

> The retainers are pretty much at the top of that ladder and I think that you have to earn each rung So you really usually start down here with free work (build-sell-grok-bots-2-hour-course.md)

> paid audits to get your foot in the door for these bigger projects that could be anywhere from 1,000 to upwards of 10,000 (build-sell-grok-bots-2-hour-course.md)

## Service Ladder: Upwork is a channel, not a rung
`[J] · [D] · uploaded 2026-08-11 · how-to-build-a-one-person-ai-business-using-claude-code.md · q45 · stable`

q45 puts Upwork on rung 0 or 1. In the video it is his second place to find buyers, after the warm network. The Upwork search keywords he gives "map directly to your service ladder" (`script-warm-outreach-trojan-horse-upwork.md`).

> the second place you go is Upwork this is where strangers go shopping for what you're selling so the intent is already there which is the whole game (how-to-build-a-one-person-ai-business-using-claude-code.md)

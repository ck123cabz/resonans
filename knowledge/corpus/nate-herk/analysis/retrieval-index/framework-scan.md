# SCAN framework

SCAN is Nate Herk's four-step method for stage 2 of his start-over video (2025-10-03): AI consulting. The letters are Study the business, Calculate the opportunity, Architect the solution, Narrate the results. It appears in that one file. Pass 2 checked whether SCAN was Devin Karns's model relabelled; it is Nate's, said in a solo video.

## SCAN: stage 2, selling clarity instead of one template
`[F] · [D] · uploaded 2025-10-03 · how-i-d-make-money-with-ai-in-2026-if-i-had-to-start-over.md · q37, q50 · stable`

After a few freelance builds (`framework-build.md`) he says the obvious next problem is that businesses do not know what to automate. The consultant audits the business and designs a roadmap. At True Horizon they still plugged in a library of templates for quick wins. He scores the stage speed to money 6, ease for beginners 4, income potential 6.

> As a consultant you're no longer just offering one automation one template You're offering clarity You audit the business find the biggest opportunities and design a road map (how-i-d-make-money-with-ai-in-2026-if-i-had-to-start-over.md)

## SCAN prices: $5,000 audit, then $20,000 to $100,000 to implement

This is a consulting-stage audit from 2025. His 2026 ladder prices a paid audit at $500 to $2,500 or $3,000 for small businesses (`framework-ai-service-ladder.md`). ## SCAN S: study the business

## SCAN C: calculate the opportunity, the $200,000 × 70% example
`[R] · [D] · uploaded 2025-10-03 · how-i-d-make-money-with-ai-in-2026-if-i-had-to-start-over.md · q42 · stable`

Turn the inefficiency into a number an owner reads. His example is generic labor.

> if a process costs $200,000 a year in labor and AI can cut it by 70% that's $140,000 savings opportunity (how-i-d-make-money-with-ai-in-2026-if-i-had-to-start-over.md)

He frames it as investment, not cost: "it doesn't become a cost to work with you it becomes an investment with a measurable return". Gemini's q42 turns this into an e-commerce brand with $200,000 of refund waste. That merges in Devin Karns's refund case from a different video (see the guest section below). Nate's example has no industry.

## SCAN A: architect the solution
`[F] · [D] · uploaded 2025-10-03 · how-i-d-make-money-with-ai-in-2026-if-i-had-to-start-over.md · q50 · stable`

> The A stands for architect the solution Design a road map Some workflows will be plug-and-play from existing templates Others will need to be built from scratch (how-i-d-make-money-with-ai-in-2026-if-i-had-to-start-over.md)

He says the system now "touches multiple departments", which is the difference from stage 1.

## SCAN N: narrate the results
`[F] · [D] · uploaded 2025-10-03 · how-i-d-make-money-with-ai-in-2026-if-i-had-to-start-over.md · q50 · stable`

Consulting case studies prove business-wide ROI, where freelance case studies only prove you can build. Publish them on your website, LinkedIn, X or YouTube.

> Finally the N stands for narrate the results Unlike the freelancer stage where your case studies prove you can build consulting case studies prove that you can deliver businesswide ROI (how-i-d-make-money-with-ai-in-2026-if-i-had-to-start-over.md)

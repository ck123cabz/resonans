# SHARE framework

SHARE is Nate Herk's five-step content funnel: Show, Hook, Attract, Recommend, Expand. It is the fourth and last stage of his start-over video of 2025-10-03, the stage he calls teaching. The name appears in that one file only. BUILD, SCAN and GROW are the other three stages of the same video.

## SHARE: where it sits in the four-stage start-over plan
`[F] · [D] · uploaded 2025-10-03 · how-i-d-make-money-with-ai-in-2026-if-i-had-to-start-over.md · q37, q50 · stable`

Stage 1 is freelancing with templates (BUILD), stage 2 is consulting (SCAN), stage 3 is an AI partner agency (GROW), stage 4 is teaching (SHARE). He scores each stage out of 10. Teaching gets speed to money 4, ease for beginners 1, income potential 10.

> teaching a automation is the endgame It's where authority proof and technical mastery converge If you followed the process from freelancer to consultant to an agency (how-i-d-make-money-with-ai-in-2026-if-i-had-to-start-over.md)

He describes the funnel in six words before naming the framework.

> But anyways that's the whole funnel Videos free school group paid group Almost all of it is pure profit (how-i-d-make-money-with-ai-in-2026-if-i-had-to-start-over.md)

In the same video he says he makes "over $150,000 per month in pure profit from teaching AI automation".

## SHARE S: show
`[F] · [D] · uploaded 2025-10-03 · how-i-d-make-money-with-ai-in-2026-if-i-had-to-start-over.md · q50 · stable`

Show automations that are unique enough to make a viewer say "wow". He adds that cookie-cutter workflows fail here because there is "no hiding if the automation isn't working".

> So the framework here is the share framework S stands for show Demonstrate automations that are genuinely unique and valuable (how-i-d-make-money-with-ai-in-2026-if-i-had-to-start-over.md)

The line "showing the destination, not just the car" that q28 and q40 attach to Show has no caption hit in a grep of 299 files; treat it as Gemini's. What he does say, on 2026-07-22 and about sales, is "You're selling the destination You're not selling the journey" (how-i-d-make-money-with-claude-if-my-life-depended-on-it.md).

## SHARE in practice: packaging and per-video resources
`[J] · [M] · no transcript · q28 · version-bound`

[M] Gemini's q28 summary; not checked line by line. Titles carry a dollar or time outcome ("I built this AI Agent in 2 hours (and got paid $1200)" on 2025-08-17, "The 3-Step System Behind My $20K/Month AI Agency Retainers" on 2026-08-25; both titles are in sources.tsv). Skills called `packaging`, `idea-mining` and a YouTube strategist agent pull outlier videos and comment pain points into daily concepts. Output grew from 30 videos a quarter through 2025 to 71 in 2026-Q2, per sources.tsv.

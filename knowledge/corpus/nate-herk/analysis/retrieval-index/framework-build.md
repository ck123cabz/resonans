# BUILD framework

BUILD is Nate Herk's five-step plan for stage 1 of his start-over video (2025-10-03): freelancing with n8n templates. The letters are Block by block, Understand the use case, Install and imitate, Land the first client, Document and duplicate. It appears in that one file. Gemini's q37 missed it; pass 2 found it next to SCAN, SHARE and GROW.

## BUILD: stage 1, freelancing with AI modules
`[F] · [D] · uploaded 2025-10-03 · how-i-d-make-money-with-ai-in-2026-if-i-had-to-start-over.md · q50 · stable`

Stage 1 sells ready-made n8n templates, customized per client. He calls templates Lego bricks: learn one block, sell it, add the next. He scores the stage speed to money 8, ease for beginners 6, income potential 3, and says income "usually caps in the mid to high four figures per month" until you move to consulting (`framework-scan.md`).

> So stage number one is freelancing with AI modules Instead of building everything from scratch you can utilize NADN templates (how-i-d-make-money-with-ai-in-2026-if-i-had-to-start-over.md)

> This is where I think you should start prioritizing reps over outcomes because it's really going to help you start to think like a business owner (how-i-d-make-money-with-ai-in-2026-if-i-had-to-start-over.md)

## BUILD U: understand the use case, with his niche examples
`[F] · [D] · uploaded 2025-10-03 · how-i-d-make-money-with-ai-in-2026-if-i-had-to-start-over.md · q42 · stable`

Match each template to the businesses it helps most. His three pairings: an AI receptionist for dentists, HVAC and med spas; an email support agent for e-commerce shops and service providers; a newsletter ghostwriter for coaches and SaaS founders.

> The U stands for understand the use case Ask what kind of businesses would this template help the most For example an AI receptionist template could help dentists HVAC med spas (how-i-d-make-money-with-ai-in-2026-if-i-had-to-start-over.md)

## BUILD I: install and imitate, then a 60-second demo
`[F] · [D] · uploaded 2025-10-03 · how-i-d-make-money-with-ai-in-2026-if-i-had-to-start-over.md · q42, q50 · stable`

Import the template, run it on test data until every step makes sense, customize it, then record a 60-second demo of the value.

> The I stands for install and imitate Import the template into NAND Run it with test data until you understand every single step that's going into it (how-i-d-make-money-with-ai-in-2026-if-i-had-to-start-over.md)

> Then build a simple 60-second demo of you showing how it works and the value that it adds (how-i-d-make-money-with-ai-in-2026-if-i-had-to-start-over.md)

He warns you cannot sell a template as-is: "You're going to be able to take a template customize it a little bit and then sell it to a business."

## BUILD worked example: Jerome's newsletter template
`[B] · [D] · uploaded 2025-10-03 · how-i-d-make-money-with-ai-in-2026-if-i-had-to-start-over.md · q29 · stable`

Jerome, an AIS community member who did not see himself as an n8n expert, took Nate's free newsletter-writing agent template. It researched a topic each morning and drafted a newsletter in the client's voice. He ran it until he understood it, tweaked the system prompts and research settings for one client's industry and brand voice, and sold it. Nate says Jerome "gave them back roughly 5 hours a week or 20 hours a month". Gemini's "90% automated" and "$10k+ saved" are [M]; the caption says "potentially tens of thousands of dollars" over a year. The result is a student's, told by Nate, and uncorroborated.

> he tweaked the system prompts a little bit and the research configuration so that the whole newsletter was tailored towards the client's industry and match their brand voice (how-i-d-make-money-with-ai-in-2026-if-i-had-to-start-over.md)

## BUILD and the era it belongs to
`[J] · [I] · uploaded 2025-10-03 · how-i-d-make-money-with-ai-in-2026-if-i-had-to-start-over.md · q36 · version-bound`

BUILD is an n8n-era plan: every step names n8n templates. The 2026 equivalent is rung 0 and rung 1 of the AI Service Ladder (`framework-ai-service-ladder.md`), where the entry product is a Claude Code or AIOS setup session. The template-first advice and the case-study habit carry across; the tool does not. This mapping is inferred, not stated by him.

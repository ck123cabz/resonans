# Student and guest case studies

The people Nate holds up as proof, 2025-09 to 2026-06. One heading per person. A guest speaking in an interview is tagged `[D:guest:Name]` and is not Nate's own claim. Every outcome here is self-reported; none is Nate's own revenue. His own early builds are in `build-early-agent-builds-ledger.md`.

## [D:guest:Christian] Student, 21, Arizona: construction PM assistant
`[B] · [D:guest:Christian][G] · uploaded 2026-02-04 · how-to-sign-your-first-ai-automation-client-in-7-days-with-proof.md · q29, q23, q45 · stable`

An AIS Plus student built a project-manager assistant for a local construction company off one of Nate's assistant templates. It sent client status updates and answered FAQs. He closed the client five days after joining, at $1,500, then raised it: "we're doing uh 2,000 now". Whether the $2,000 is monthly is not stated. He posted the win on a Friday; three days later another member hired him as an "AI automator", and he went full-time.

Before that he had tried cold email at 450 a day off LinkedIn lists through Instantly and got no replies.

> I was doing a bunch of emails like 450 a day but uh it was like a crazy open rate but nobody wa[s responding] (how-to-sign-your-first-ai-automation-client-in-7-days-with-proof.md)

> we actually we upped it recently so we're doing uh 2,000 now (how-to-sign-your-first-ai-automation-client-in-7-days-with-proof.md)

Warning lines: the 450-a-day failure is Christian's, never Sav's. q45's claim that AIS Plus members "frequently" close pilots in 5 to 7 days rests on this one student. Nate's lesson (q29, [M]): sell as a guide, not a template salesman.

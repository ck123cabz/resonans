## VAM: the Value Acceleration Method

**Type:** FRM · **Prov:** [M*] · **Scale:** Acquisition.com portfolio · **Signals:** S-102.1 · **Files:** q102

Acquisition.com's core growth framework. It analyses customer value data to cut channels that bring low-value buyers and double down on channels that bring high-LTV buyers. $100M Lost Chapters, intro chapter / chapter 2. No other layer-two answer names VAM. It sits in the same chapter as the first-avatar process (q84: score current customers by value, find underserved high-value veins, identify common qualifiers, run tailored acquisition), so it may be the name for that process.

## Value-to-price ratio: 10:1

**Type:** NUM · **Prov:** [M*] · **Scale:** general · **Signals:** S-103.1 · **Files:** q103

The offer should deliver at least 10x perceived value against the price charged (e.g. $10,000 in perceived value for a $1,000 price), to create an irresistible price-to-value gap. Probably from $100M Offers, not layer two; no layer-two lane answer holds it. Layer one: `pricing-and-tiers.md` § Premium pricing: the 10x value-to-price gap.

## Square root law of team productivity

**Type:** NUM · **Prov:** [M*] · **Scale:** an organisation with a team · **Signals:** S-103.5 · **Files:** q103

## Speed to lead: 60 seconds to 5 minutes, 391%

**Type:** NUM · **Prov:** [M*] · **Scale:** a business with inbound leads · **Signals:** S-103.7 · **Files:** q103

## Rule of 100: the forms q103 gives

**Type:** NUM · **Prov:** [M*] · **Scale:** a business with no paid reach · **Signals:** S-108.2 · **Files:** q103, q108

100 primary marketing actions every day: 100 cold outreaches a day, 100 minutes of content creation a day, $100 a day in ad spend, or 100 warm outreach messages a day. 100 days is the least time to run the main marketing actions before judging or changing a channel. q108 adds: never set a daily cold outreach goal below 100 attempts, for 100 days minimum. ## $1 million as 100 clients at $10,000

**Type:** NUM, CLM · **Prov:** [M*] · **Scale:** high-ticket, $10,000 per customer · **Signals:** S-108.9 · **Files:** q103, q108

q103: hitting $1M in revenue is much easier by getting 100 clients at $10,000 ($200/month over 4 years) than by finding 10,000 people to buy a $100 product. q108: reaching 100 people a day ("$700/week") brings 2 customers a week willing to spend $10,000/year (or $200/month over 4 years); 100 customers at $10,000/year is a $1,000,000/year business from 100 daily reach-outs alone.

Flags: $200 x 48 months = $9,600, not $10,000. "$10,000/year" and "$10,000 over 4 years" cannot both be right; q103 gives $10,000 per client in total. "$700/week" is unexplained next to 100 a day; it may be 700 reach-outs a week garbled into dollars. 2 a week reaches 100 customers in about 50 weeks. Pass 2 L2.4 #24: a projection, not usable.

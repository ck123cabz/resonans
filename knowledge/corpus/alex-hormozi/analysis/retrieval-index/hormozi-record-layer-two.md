**Type:** BIO, CLM · **Prov:** [M*] · **Scale:** one gym to six · **Signals:** S-101.1, S-101.2, S-101.4, S-101.7, S-101.8 · **Files:** q101

Monthly rent was $4,972. The figures add up ($50,000 − $10,000 − $34,000 = $6,000); layer one left the $6,000 unaccounted for.
- **CLM** First ad campaign: $1,000 on Facebook ads brought in $5,700 by selling 19 members on a 6-week challenge at $299 each. 19 x $299 = $5,681.

Grew to 5 to 6 locations over 3 years and sold them in 2016. A first-month figure exactly equal to the rent is worth checking against the audio; his own records are not an independent record.

- **CLM** In his first challenge, only 15% of clients hit their weight-loss goals because the meal plans were too complex. After he simplified the plans and added strict accountability, about 80% did.

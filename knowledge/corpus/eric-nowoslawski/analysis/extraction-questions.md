# NotebookLM Extraction Questions — Eric Nowoslawski Corpus
**Purpose:** Systematically extract Eric's cold email methodology from 242 YouTube videos via targeted queries.
**Constraint:** 50 queries/day. Designed for ~35-40 questions total (one session with buffer).

---

## Category 1: Email Sequence Architecture (5 questions)

1. **What are ALL of Eric's distinct cold email sequence structures? For each, give: number of emails, threading strategy (which are threaded vs. net-new), and the strategic purpose of each email position. Include any structures beyond the standard 3-4 step, the "creative ideas" campaign, and the "building it for you" lead magnet sequence.**

2. **What is Eric's complete framework for Email 1 (the initial spear) across all sequence types? Include: the exact line-by-line structure, what each line must accomplish, common mistakes he warns against, and at least 3 different examples from different industries.**

3. **What is Eric's complete framework for follow-up emails (Email 2, 3, 4)? For each position: what changes between threaded vs. net-new emails, what content belongs where, how to "change the angle" between emails, and the breakup email formula.**

4. **What does Eric say about sequence length and timing? Include: optimal number of emails, days between sends, time of day to send, which day of the week performs best, and his reasoning for why long sequences are dead.**

5. **What does Eric say about A/B testing within sequences? How should you test subject lines, body copy, CTAs, and offers? What sample sizes does he recommend? How do you know when you have "message-market fit"?**

## Category 2: Subject Lines & Opening Lines (4 questions)

6. **What are ALL of Eric's subject line formulas and rules? Include: his "internal camouflage" concept, the 2-3 word rule, specific examples organized by type (question-based, name-drop, pattern interrupt, etc.), and subject lines he explicitly says NOT to use.**

7. **What is Eric's "recency waterfall" framework for opening lines? List every trigger type in order of priority (LinkedIn post, new hire, funding round, etc.), with examples of how to turn each trigger into an opening line.**

8. **What does Eric say about personalization in the first line? Include: AI personalization vs. manual, how to use Clay for personalization at scale, what counts as "good" vs. "creepy" personalization, and the balance between personalization effort and reply rate.**

9. **What opening line patterns does Eric explicitly warn against? What are the cliches, the overused templates, and the patterns that immediately signal "this is a sales email"?**

## Category 3: Calls to Action & Offers (3 questions)

12. **What does Eric say about using lead magnets, free resources, and "building it for you" as CTAs? When is this appropriate vs. overkill? What types of lead magnets work in cold email vs. inbound?**

# Why Nations Fail Ep 1, long cut, 13 October 2026

Project: `video-projects/wnf-ep01-long/` (its twin `wnf-ep01-short/` and the shared bed and effects in
`wnf-ep01-shared/`). A 3:55 9:16 Taglish history Reel for Sai, voiced by ElevenLabs Sarah. `VERIFY.md` there
has every check and both blind-read rounds.

- Christian asked for an A/B test of three scripts, watched the first animatic, and then set a goal of a
  production-ready long cut. Build the animatic so the production pass reuses it: the v1 driver, captions and
  timing carried straight into v2.
- Maps come from Natural Earth through `world-atlas` and `d3-geo`, projected once into `src/maps.js`. A
  bounding box drawn as a d3 polygon must wind clockwise (west edge first), or the projection fits the rest of
  the globe and the map comes out empty.
- `tools/layout.mjs` (moved into the kit on 13 Oct as `scripts/check-layout.mjs`, run as `npm run layout -- video-projects/wnf-ep01-long --band 1180`) seeks three times per voice line in headless Chrome and measures each element's drawn ink
  (text runs and leaf shapes), not its box: full-width text boxes gave 101 false hits, ink gave the 9 real ones.
  Full-bleed backgrounds carry `data-bleed`.
- `hyperframes check` reads text over an SVG shape as text over the page behind it (1.21:1 on the Congress
  label). Giving the label its own background fixed the measurement and changed nothing visible.
- Scribe on the whole narration misheard lines that it heard cleanly one at a time; judge a line by its own
  clip. `tools/retake.mjs` voices seeded takes and keeps the clearest; pass `null`, not `undefined`, for the
  default take, or JavaScript fills in the current pick.
- ElevenLabs sound effects: three of twelve came back near silent (peaks under -30 dB). Gated LUFS misreads
  short hits, so `gen.mjs` checks the peak.
- Blind readers on one frame per line found what every frame check passed: a scorecard too small for a phone,
  a "JAPAN" label read as a place in Luzon, a crowd that contradicted "karamihan", a Spaniard whose helmet read
  as a salakot. A historian reader caught two claims the claims table had passed as simplifications.

## The animation pass, afternoon of 13 October

- Christian heard "rushed" at 168 words a minute with 0.3 s gaps. ElevenLabs' own `speed: 0.9` plus written
  beats (`[pause n]` before a line, `[n]` inside one, which `narrate.mjs` voices as separate clips) fixed it
  without stretching audio; he picked 0.9 from a 30-second A/B of the opening.
- English source cards over a Taglish voice became the thing viewers scrolled at. Cards that covered the
  picture they cited were the blocker; as footer chips with the highlighted phrase at 42 px they passed.
  Never put a quote on paper that is a paraphrase.
- A middle third told by documents was fixed by one stage that changes state line by line, not by better
  cards: the designer reader's verdict went from "no" to "the storytelling problem is solved" on that change.
- A camera over a world of cells breaks a layout check that assumes every scene fills the frame; check only
  the cell the camera has settled on. Anything fixed to the screen (the scorecard) must leave before a pan
  and arrive after it, never move while the camera does.
- One cast decided early (landlord = bowler hat) would have saved a review round.

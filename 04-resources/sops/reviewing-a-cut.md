# Reviewing a cut

**Use this when:** a render with a new version stamp (`renders/<slug>-v8e-share.mp4`) arrives in the
project folder.

**You're done when:** Christian has `qa/<version>/review.md`, where every row carries a time, what was
seen or heard, the pack call it breaks, and a concrete change.

> [!info]+ Header
> - **Kind:** field. **Follower:** Sai on a first run, or an agent working in `content-studio/`.
> - **Mode:** read-do. **Runs:** every render round. **A miss costs:** Christian finds it himself a round later.

> [!quote]+ Source
> The video-craft immersion pack, `knowledge/learning/video-craft/judgment.md`, calls 5, 7, 8, 10, 12,
> 16, 18, 19, 20 and 21. Christian's own rules from `content-studio/LEARNINGS.md` and
> the video playbook (not published). The steps are drafted from the Ep 1 review record
> (`wnf/ep01-long/qa/v8d/items.md` and the retro, `docs/reviews/2026-10-15-wnf-ep01-retro.md`).
> Christian's walk of one real cut is still to come, and it rewrites any step it contradicts.

> [!todo]+ When a new stamped render arrives
> **10 min · the project folder open, VERIFY.md and the share copy**
>
> **Do:** pin down which render you are reviewing and what it has to say.
>
> **Why:** a relayed "too abrupt" note once came from v9 or earlier, and nobody could tell which
> (`revise-video`, step 1).
>
> **Stuck:** [[knowledge/learning/video-craft/judgment|Video-craft judgment]], call 2
>
> 1. **Create `qa/<version>/review.md` and write the file name, version and run time on line one.**
>    Check: a line like "wnf-ep01-long-v8e-share.mp4 · v8e · 5:49".
> 2. **Copy the video's one thing from SCRIPT.md under that line, word for word.** Check: one sentence
>    of under 20 words; if SCRIPT.md has none, take the exit below.
> 3. **Copy three numbers from the top section of VERIFY.md: shortest hold, layout hits, true peak.**
>    Because these are measured, so your eye can skip them. Check: a line like "hold 1.6 s · layout 0
>    at gap 40 · -2.1 dBTP".
> 4. **Copy the share copy to your phone and play the first frame.** Check: the phone shows the same
>    run time as line one.

> [!todo]+ When the share copy plays on your phone
> **15 min · phone at arm's length, earphones in**
>
> **Do:** watch it the way a viewer will, before you know where the problems are.
>
> **Why:** Xatse checks a cut at its final compression on a phone (call 21), and three fresh readers
> each called a 19 px chapter bar unreadable on a phone on 14 Oct (call 16).
>
> **Stuck:** [[knowledge/learning/video-craft/judgment|Video-craft judgment]], calls 5 and 21
>
> 1. **Play the first 2 seconds muted, stop, and write what the video is about.** Check: a line after
>    "muted 2 s:"; if it does not match the one thing, that is row 1.
> 2. **Write what fills the first second.** Because a logo, a fade from black or a greeting there
>    loses the viewer (call 5). Check: a line after "first second:", such as "headline, 1960 chart".
> 3. **Watch the whole cut once with sound, without pausing.** Check: "watched once, no pauses, 5:49"
>    written under the first second line.
> 4. **Write each time you looked away, got lost or squinted, right after the watch.** Check: rows
>    like "1:31 · lost: which map is this?".
> 5. **Watch it again, pausing, and write one row per problem: time, what you see or hear, the call.**
>    Check: every row reads like "0:57 · two maps on screen 1.2 s · call 10".

> [!todo]+ When the second watch is done
> **20 min · the phone, the pack's judgment file beside it**
>
> **Do:** walk the cut segment by segment for what one viewing misses.
>
> **Why:** blind readers on frames found what every frame check passed, on four projects
> (LEARNINGS), and Christian's v6 notes asked for a headline and one job per segment (call 7).
>
> **Stuck:** [[knowledge/learning/video-craft/judgment|Video-craft judgment]], calls 7, 10, 12 and 16
>
> 1. **Pause on each segment's first frame and write its headline.** Check: one headline per
>    segment, such as "Maraming dahilan ng agwat"; a segment with none becomes a row.
> 2. **Write each segment's job in one word: feel, trust or laugh.** Check: one word per segment; a
>    segment you cannot name becomes a row.
> 3. **Write each hold over 3 seconds where nothing on screen changes.** Check: rows like "2:08 ·
>    same map 4 s, nothing moves · call 10", or "long holds: none".
> 4. **Write each move you cannot give a reason for in one line.** Check: rows like "1:16 · title
>    spins, points at nothing · call 12", or "moves: all have a reason".
> 5. **Read every piece of on-screen text at arm's length.** Check: rows naming the time and the text
>    you could not read, or "text: all read".
> 6. **Listen at each voice line's start and end for a click, a jump in loudness, or a wrong stress.**
>    Because Scribe passes lamang, kita, upa, ani and lupa when the stress is wrong; only a Tagalog ear
>    catches them (PLAYBOOK). Check: rows with the time and the word, or "audio: none heard".

> [!todo]+ When your own rows are written
> **30 min · two people who have not seen the script, one phone**
>
> **Do:** find out whether a stranger gets the one thing before anyone polishes the picture.
>
> **Why:** Cal.com's viewers could not tell where each character was until a map went in, and a
> designer blind reader's "no" to Christian's middle third led to one moving stage (call 8).
>
> **Stuck:** [[knowledge/learning/video-craft/judgment|Video-craft judgment]], call 8
>
> 1. **Show the cut to two people who have not seen the script, on the phone, and say nothing while it
>    plays.** An agent uses one fresh reader per contact sheet (`npm run sheet`), given no brief.
>    Check: two names, or two reader ids, and today's date in review.md.
> 2. **Ask each reader, after: "What was it about?" and "Where did you lose it?"** Check: their
>    answers written word for word under their names.
> 3. **Compare each answer to the one thing on line two.** Because a miss sends the work back to
>    story, ahead of every picture note (call 8). Check: "reader 1: matches" or "reader 1: misses",
>    and a miss becomes the top row, tagged "story".

> [!todo]+ When the blind reads are in
> **10 min · review.md**
>
> **Do:** turn the rows into changes Christian can say yes or no to.
>
> **Why:** "None of them is fixed by adding a gradient" (Chordiya, call 19), and Christian decides
> whether a draft is good (`revise-video`, "His calls").
>
> **Stuck:** [[knowledge/learning/video-craft/judgment|Video-craft judgment]], calls 19 and 20
>
> 1. **Rewrite each feeling word ("flat", "abrupt", "pop") as a change to one element.** Check: rows
>    like "0:57 · maps hold 1.2 s · call 10 · hold both maps 2 s".
> 2. **Order the rows: story first, then sound and script, then picture.** Because a revision round
>    takes sound and script first and picture changes wait for the next one (`revise-video`). Check:
>    every "story" row sits above every picture row.
> 3. **End each row with "fact" or "taste".** Because a push that is only your taste loses its weight
>    (call 20). Check: a row's last word is "fact" (measured, or a reader's words) or "taste".
> 4. **Send review.md to Christian with the version and the row count in the first line.** Check: a
>    sent message whose first line reads like "v8e review · 14 rows · 1 story".

> [!danger]+ Never
> - **Never call a cut good or ready.** No gate: whether a draft is good is Christian's call (`revise-video`, "His calls").
> - **Never edit the build, the script or a render while reviewing.** No gate: the review's only output is review.md.
> - **Never recommend a visual change on your own look alone.** No gate: Christian's rule since 6 Oct is a blind read before any visual recommendation, so step 4's readers come first.
> - **Never judge text size or framing on a monitor.** No gate: the phone at arm's length is the test (call 16).
> - **Never propose time-stretching the voice to fix length.** No gate: Christian's rule, length moves with words and pauses (LEARNINGS).

> [!tip]+ Exits
> - The render has no version stamp: escalate to Christian with the file name and the time it arrived.
> - SCRIPT.md states no one thing: escalate to Christian with the project name, before any watch.
> - VERIFY.md has no hold, layout or true-peak number: pause and log the missing number in review.md,
>   then escalate to Christian with the version and what is missing.
> - Both readers miss the one thing: escalate to Christian with both quotes and your recommendation to
>   go back to story before any picture row.
> - A row reverses a note Christian approved in an earlier version: escalate to Christian with both
>   notes and their versions, and work no further on that row (call 18).

> [!warning]+ Where it goes wrong
> - A check covers part of the video and the report says all of it: v8c reported every picture held
>   1.5 s, and one held 1.2 s (Ep 1 retro).
> - A note applied in part comes back: "Maraming dahilan" got a voice line on v7 and no heading, so the
>   v6 note returned on v8 (Ep 1 retro). Write what the frame should show, not only what is wrong.
> - A new hook gets asked for when viewers leave all the way down; that is the body (call 5).

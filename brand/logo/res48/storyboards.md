# Resonans brand film, four storyboard angles

Christian asked on 21 Oct for a compilation of moments where something finds its signal: radio tuning, a TV
set finding the picture, a radio finding the channel. Each angle below is about 15 seconds, cut for a 9:16
reel and reusable as the 16:9 site hero. All four end on the same card, so the angle only changes what comes
before it.

Drawn and animated on 21 Oct in HyperFrames, no AI generation: `content-studio/video-projects/resonans/storyboards/`
(`node build.mjs` rebuilds it from `src/`, `node tools/board.mjs` lays out `boards/angle-1.png` to `angle-4.png`
from the hero snapshots). All four angles play back to back in one 62-second animatic.

The end card in every angle: the noise collapses into one steady line, which becomes the mark, then
"Marketing works when it gets understood." and the name. The mark itself is still open; the steady line
stands in for it.

## Angle 1: Finding the signal

His three moments plus two from now, so it reads as today, not as nostalgia.

| # | Time | Picture | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 to 2 s | A car radio dial sweeping; the needle drifts across stations | static, half-words |
| 2 | 2 to 4 s | A walkie-talkie's channel number flips 14, 15, 16 | crackle, "do you copy?" |
| 3 | 4 to 6 s | An old TV in static, a face flickering in and out | hiss |
| 4 | 6 to 8 s | A video call frozen on a blurred face, "Reconnecting" | a voice cutting in and out |
| 5 | 8 to 11 s | All four in a grid; on one frame they all lock: station, channel, picture, call | the noise drops to one clean tone |
| 6 | 11 to 15 s | The tone draws one steady line, then the line and the name | silence under the line |

## Angle 2: Falling into sync

People and things finding one rhythm. The nuance is that nobody gets louder; they line up.

| # | Time | Picture | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 to 3 s | An orchestra tuning before the concert, close-ups of bows and keys | every instrument at once |
| 2 | 3 to 5 s | Metronomes on a shared board, each ticking out of step | scattered ticking |
| 3 | 5 to 7 s | A crowd clapping, all off the beat | scattered claps |
| 4 | 7 to 9 s | The oboe plays one A; the room goes quiet to listen | one note |
| 5 | 9 to 12 s | The orchestra tunes to that note; the metronomes fall into step; the clapping finds one beat | one shared pulse |
| 6 | 12 to 15 s | The pulse draws the steady line, then the line and the name | the held note fades |

## Angle 3: The edit

Christian's own sequence, led by the words. It shows the work Resonans does: the long version cut to one
line a customer can repeat.

| # | Time | Picture | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 to 3 s | The screen fills with the long gate 1 and 2 text, overlapping, notifications landing on top | pings, chatter |
| 2 | 3 to 6 s | A cursor strikes words out, fast, one after another | keys, deletes |
| 3 | 6 to 8 s | One line is left: "Marketing works when it gets understood." | the room goes quiet |
| 4 | 8 to 10 s | The line shakes like a signal in static | low hiss |
| 5 | 10 to 12 s | The shaking settles into one steady line | a clean tone |
| 6 | 12 to 15 s | The line and the name | the tone holds |

## Angle 4: One of each sense

One moment from hearing, seeing, moving and understanding, closing on the orchestra, so it covers more
ground than angle 1 without the nostalgia.

| # | Time | Picture | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 to 2 s | Hearing: a phone held up in a loud bar, Shazam listening | bar noise |
| 2 | 2 to 4 s | Seeing: a camera hunting for focus on a face | lens whir |
| 3 | 4 to 6 s | Moving: a crowd's clapping, off the beat | scattered claps |
| 4 | 6 to 8 s | Understanding: scrambled letters on screen | soft clicks |
| 5 | 8 to 11 s | All lock on one frame: song found, face sharp, clap on the beat, letters read "understood" | everything drops to one note |
| 6 | 11 to 15 s | The orchestra holds that note, it draws the steady line, then the line and the name | the held note |

## How they compare

| Angle | Reads in a second | Nuance | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Finding the signal | yes, everyone has tuned a radio | low: devices, not people | reads as tech or telecom |
| 2 Falling into sync | yes once the oboe plays | high: nobody gets louder, they line up | slower to start |
| 3 The edit | yes, people know a cursor | high: it is the actual work | text on screen, weak without sound off |
| 4 One of each sense | mostly | medium | four set-ups in 8 seconds is busy |

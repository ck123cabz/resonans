The mark is sand on a ringing plate.

Scatter sand on a brass plate and bow its edge. At a resonant pitch the sand slides off the moving parts and settles into one figure. Resonans uses the figure for mode 1 · 5.

Fig. 2 · Construction

Computed from the plate, drawn at letter weight

cos(πx/2)·cos(5πy/2) + cos(5πx/2)·cos(πy/2) = 0
  1. The equation gives every point of the plate that stays still in mode 1 · 5.
  2. Rays from the centre cross those still lines. The first crossing makes the core, the second makes the ring.
  3. 48 crossings set the core and 96 set the ring, so both have exact four-fold symmetry.
  4. Both curves take a stroke of 16 units, the same stroke as the letters.

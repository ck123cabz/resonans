Fig. 2 · Construction

One stroke in four parts

knot → stem → arch → point → line · pen 8 · shake 3.5 → 0
  1. The knot is three loops of different sizes whose centres wander. Random seed 20 draws this one.
  2. The stem climbs out of the knot into an arch 100 units wide and 36 tall, flat like the sketch.
  3. The arch drops to a point, and the line kicks up from just above it.
  4. The pen shakes by up to 3.5 units until the long line, which runs dead straight.

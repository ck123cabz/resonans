# The offer

Agreed 21 Oct 2026 in an offer grilling between Christian and Sai.

## Who it's for

Small businesses in the US, Australia, Canada and the UK, any industry until the first send
run names one. Local businesses in the Philippines get 1 send in 5, through the warm network.

## What Resonans does

Resonans acts as the client's marketing department. Christian said on 20 Oct: "We would act
like their marketing department." Videos are AI-assisted, never fully generated: built in
HyperFrames from the client's own material, faceless by default. Stills, the brand kit,
posting, captions and a monthly report sit around them.

Resonans promises output: videos on set dates, up to 2 revisions each. It reports sales
and makes no promise about them.

## Packages

Priced by package, never by hours. Christian on 20 Oct: "I don't want to offer hours. This
keeps me as an employee."

| Package | Contents |
|---|---|
| 1 | 1 long-form video a week, 1 reel a day, stills, brand kit, monthly report |
| 2 | 2 long-form a week, 2 reels a day, 6 stills a day |
| 3 | the full volume plus comment and DM replies, founder recording edits and a weekly call; 2 clients at most |

Counts and prices get set from a one-week volume run on Resonans's own channels, 2 to 8 Nov 2026.

## How a client starts

1. Message 1 carries a public audit of their marketing, a content strategy and keyword
   research. Follow-ups go out on days 1, 4 and 8.
2. Before any free work, Resonans asks the money question: if it works, could they spend
   on this every month?
3. A yes gets a free trial: their brand kit finished and 1 video with its creative brief, on
   three terms. They use it, give feedback and leave a review, and Resonans may use it as a
   case study.
4. The paid first month starts the volume.

## Out of scope

Filming by default (a client's own recording is optional, in package 3), paid ads, and
work for anyone's employer as proof.
